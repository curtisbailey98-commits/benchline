# Benchline Google Review Ask Flow
## Earn reviews without sounding pushy

**Principle:** Only ask after a clear win. Never ask while a complaint is open.

---

### Step 1 — Confirm satisfaction (verbal or text)

> "Before I take off — is there anything you'd want done differently?"

If anything is off → fix it. Do **not** ask for a review.

If they're happy → Step 2.

---

### Step 2 — Soft ask + direct link

Hand a card or text:

> Awesome — that means a lot. If you have 30 seconds, a Google review helps other neighbors find reliable help. Here's my link: [YOUR_GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK]  
> No pressure if you're busy.

**Tips**
- Short link or QR on a leave-behind card
- Mention "neighbors" not "algorithm"
- Ask once; maybe one gentle reminder at 24h if they said yes but forgot

---

### 24h reminder (only if they agreed)

> Hey [Name] — no rush, just the review link again in case it got buried: [LINK]. Thanks either way.

---

### What never to do
- Gate discounts on reviews
- Write the review for them
- Ask during a price dispute
- Spam the same customer weekly

---

### Referral add-on (after a 5-star)

> If anyone on your street needs [service], send them my way — I take care of referrals.

*Benchline Core Kit — replace link; print QR on job closeout card.*

# One-Time Service Agreement

> **Not legal advice.** This is a plain-language starting template for solo home-service businesses. Contract, consumer-protection, and liability laws vary by state and city. **Have a licensed attorney in your state review and adapt it before you use it.**

---

**Business:** [Business name] · [license/registration # if required] · [address] · [phone] · [email]
**Client:** [Name] · [service address] · [phone] · [email]
**Service date / window:** [___] **Estimate / quote #:** [___] (attached)

## 1. Work to be performed
We'll perform the services described in the attached estimate ("Work"). Anything not listed isn't included. Changes require a written change order (text/email approval is acceptable) that states the added price.

## 2. Price
- Total: $[___] (plus applicable tax).
- Deposit: $[___] due to book [only if used; see Deposit & Cancellation Policy].
- Balance: due on completion by [card / ACH / cash / other].

## 3. Client responsibilities
- Provide safe access to the work area at the scheduled time, plus [water / electricity / parking] as needed.
- Tell us about hazards, fragile or valuable items, pets, and known problems (leaks, loose fixtures, damaged surfaces) before we start.
- Secure pets and keep children away from the work area.

## 4. Pre-existing conditions
We'll note visible existing conditions before starting (see Property Condition Waiver). We're not responsible for pre-existing conditions or for damage resulting from conditions that weren't reasonably visible.

## 5. Scheduling, weather, and delays
We'll arrive within the agreed window. If weather, illness, or other circumstances outside our control prevent work, we'll reschedule at no charge to either party.

## 6. Cancellation
See the attached Deposit & Cancellation Policy. [Add any cooling-off/right-to-cancel language required in your state for home-service contracts.]

## 7. Satisfaction
Report any issue within [24/48] hours of completion. We'll return to correct work that doesn't match the estimate, per our Satisfaction & Re-do Policy.

## 8. Warranty
[Cleaning/lawn/washing: No warranty beyond our Satisfaction & Re-do Policy.]
[Repairs: Workmanship warrantied for [90] days; excludes client-supplied materials, misuse, and pre-existing conditions.]

## 9. Damage and insurance
We carry general liability insurance [carrier; proof on request]. Report damage you believe we caused within [48] hours with photos. If caused by our negligence, we'll repair, replace, or reimburse reasonable costs, at our option, up to [the actual cash value of the item / $___]. [Attorney: confirm any limitation of liability is enforceable in your state.]

## 10. Photos
We may photograph the work for our records. Public use requires the client's permission (see Photo & Media Release).

## 11. Payment and late fees
See Payment Terms & Late Fees.

## 12. Entire agreement
This agreement, the estimate, any change orders, and the referenced policies are the whole agreement. If any part is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. [State] law governs.

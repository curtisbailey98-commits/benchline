# Photo & Media Release

> **Not legal advice.** This is a plain-language starting template for solo home-service businesses. Contract, consumer-protection, and liability laws vary by state and city. **Have a licensed attorney in your state review and adapt it before you use it.**

Before/after photos are the best marketing a solo operator has. Get written permission before posting anything from a client's property or vehicle.

---

**Business:** [Business name]
**Client:** [Name] · [address / vehicle]
**Date:** [___]

I give [Business name] permission to take photos and short videos of the work performed at my property / on my vehicle, and to use them as selected below:

☐ **Business records only** (no public use)
☐ **Website and online listings** (Google Business Profile, directories)
☐ **Social media** (Facebook, Instagram, Nextdoor, etc.)
☐ **Printed marketing** (flyers, cards)

**Privacy protections the business will follow:**
- No house numbers, street signs, license plates, faces, family photos, mail, or personal documents in published images (blurred or cropped out).
- No exact address or identifying location published.
- ☐ I'm OK being tagged or named. ☐ Please don't tag or name me.

**Revoking permission:** I can withdraw permission at any time by text or email. The business will stop new use and remove posts it controls within [7] days; it can't recall printed materials already distributed.

No payment is owed for this permission.

---

Client: ______________________ Date: __________

> Hi [Name], would it be OK if I shared before/after photos of your [driveway / kitchen / car] on my website and social pages? I always crop out house numbers, plates, faces, and anything personal. Totally fine to say no — reply YES or NO.

# Payment Terms, Late Fees & Collections Sequence

> **Not legal advice.** This is a plain-language starting template for solo home-service businesses. Contract, consumer-protection, and liability laws vary by state and city. **Have a licensed attorney in your state review and adapt it before you use it.** Many states limit late fees and interest, and collections are regulated — confirm with your attorney.

---

## Payment terms (paste into estimates and agreements)

- **One-time jobs:** payment due on completion, before we leave, by [card / ACH / Zelle / cash / check].
- **Recurring service:** card on file charged after each visit **or** invoiced [weekly / monthly], due within **[7]** days.
- **Larger projects:** [deposit]% to book, balance on completion. [Progress payments for multi-day jobs: __% at __.]
- **Card processing:** [We don't add card fees / A __% card fee applies where allowed by law and card network rules — check before adding surcharges.]
- **Returned payments:** $[__] fee [as permitted by law].
- **Late payments:** invoices unpaid after **[14]** days incur a late fee of $[__] [or __% per month], as permitted by law. Recurring service may be paused until the account is current.

## Collections sequence (polite → firm)

| Day | Action | Message |
|-----|--------|---------|
| 0 | Invoice sent | *"Thanks, [Name]! Your invoice for [job] is here: [link]. Due [date]."* |
| 3 | Friendly nudge (if due on receipt) | *"Hi [Name], just a reminder your invoice ([link]) is still open. Thanks!"* |
| 7 | Reminder | *"Hi [Name], following up on the $[__] invoice for [job] from [date]: [link]. Let me know if there's any issue with it."* |
| 14 | Late notice | *"Hi [Name], the [job] invoice ($[__]) is now 14 days past due. Per our agreement, a $[__] late fee applies after [date]. Please pay here: [link], or call me if you'd like to set up a payment plan."* |
| 21 | Call | Call — don't text. Ask what's going on; offer a plan (e.g., two payments). Confirm in writing. |
| 30 | Final notice (email + text) | *"[Name], this is a final notice for $[__] (invoice [#], [date]). If it's not paid by [date + 10], I'll need to pursue other options, including small claims court. I'd much rather resolve this directly — please reach out."* |
| 40+ | Decide | Small claims court, a collections agency, or write it off. Stop recurring service. |

## Prevention (better than collections)
- Card on file for recurring clients (charged after each visit).
- Deposits for first-time clients on large jobs.
- Collect before you leave the job — "Are you paying by card or Zelle today?"
- Written estimate + change orders = fewer "I didn't agree to that" disputes.

# Deposit, Rescheduling & Cancellation Policy

> **Not legal advice.** This is a plain-language starting template for solo home-service businesses. Contract, consumer-protection, and liability laws vary by state and city. **Have a licensed attorney in your state review and adapt it before you use it.**

Share this policy with every estimate and link to it from your booking page. Short, clear, and applied consistently.

---

## Deposits

| Job type | Deposit |
|----------|---------|
| Recurring service | None — card on file |
| One-time jobs under $[___] | None |
| One-time jobs over $[___], first-time clients, or peak dates | [25–50]% to book |
| Special-order materials | Materials cost up front |

- Deposits are applied to the final invoice.
- [Check whether your state limits deposit size for home-service or home-improvement work.]

## Rescheduling

- **Free** with at least **[48] hours'** notice.
- With **[24–48] hours'** notice: free once; after that, treated as a late cancellation.
- We may reschedule for weather or safety at no charge to you.

## Late cancellations and no-shows

| Situation | Fee |
|-----------|-----|
| Cancel with less than [24] hours' notice | [50]% of the job price or $[___] (deposit may be kept) |
| No-show / no access / lockout at the scheduled time | [Full visit price / $___] after we wait [15] minutes and attempt to contact you |
| Job can't proceed because site isn't ready (no water/power, area not cleared) | $[___] trip fee |

## Refunds of deposits

- Cancel with [48+] hours' notice: deposit refunded in full [minus non-returnable materials already purchased].
- Late cancellation: deposit applied to the late cancellation fee.
- If **we** cancel and can't reschedule to a date that works for you: full refund.

## How to cancel or reschedule
Text or email [phone/email]. Changes count from when we receive your message.

## Why we have this policy (optional client-facing paragraph)

> "We're a small business, and each appointment is time we've held just for you — often turning away other clients to do it. This policy keeps our prices fair for everyone and lets us give you the reliable scheduling you expect."

## Scripts

**Applying a fee kindly**
> Hi [Name], sorry we couldn't make today work. Per our cancellation policy, a $[__] late-cancellation fee applies — I've added it to your next invoice. Let's get you rebooked: I have [options].

**Waiving it once (your choice)**
> I've waived the fee this time since it's the first time — just a heads-up that it applies going forward.

## 2. Price and billing
- Price per visit: $[___].
- Billing: ☐ card on file charged after each visit ☐ invoiced [weekly/monthly], due within [__] days.
- **Price changes:** we'll give at least **30 days' written notice** of any price change. You may end service before the new price takes effect.

## 3. Skipped and rescheduled visits
- **You skip:** please give [24/48] hours' notice. Skips with less notice may be charged [50]% of the visit price.
- **Lockouts / no access:** if we can't access the property at the scheduled time, the visit may be charged [in full / $___].
- **We reschedule** (weather, illness, holidays): no charge; we'll offer the nearest available day.
- **Extended pauses:** pauses longer than [4] weeks may release your recurring time slot.

## 4. Access, keys, and codes
- Keys are labeled with a code, never your address, and stored securely.
- Please update us when codes change.

## 5. Condition changes
Recurring prices assume the property stays in similar condition. If the scope grows significantly (e.g., new pets, renovations, overgrown yard after a long pause), we'll discuss a price adjustment before the next visit.

## 6. Satisfaction
Report missed items within **24 hours** of a visit; we'll correct them at no charge per our Satisfaction & Re-do Policy.

## 7. Damage and insurance
We carry general liability insurance [carrier; proof on request]. Report damage within [48] hours. See the One-Time Service Agreement §9 for how damage claims are handled [or restate here].

## 8. Non-solicitation (only if you have staff)
[Optional.] You agree not to directly hire our team members for [6] months after this agreement ends. [Confirm enforceability in your state.]

## 9. Term and ending service
- This agreement continues until either party ends it with **[14]** days' written notice (text/email is fine).
- Completed visits remain payable. Prepaid, unused visits are refunded.
- **No automatic long-term commitment.** [If you add a fixed term or auto-renewal, check your state's automatic-renewal disclosure laws.]

## 10. General
This agreement and any referenced policies are the whole agreement. [State] law governs.

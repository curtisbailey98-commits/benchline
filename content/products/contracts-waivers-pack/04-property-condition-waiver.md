# Pre-Existing Condition Acknowledgment & Limited Release

> **Not legal advice.** This is a plain-language starting template for solo home-service businesses. Contract, consumer-protection, and liability laws vary by state and city. **Have a licensed attorney in your state review and adapt it before you use it.** Releases generally **cannot** excuse your own negligence, and some states restrict them for consumer services.

Complete on site **before** work begins, with the client present. Attach "before" photos.

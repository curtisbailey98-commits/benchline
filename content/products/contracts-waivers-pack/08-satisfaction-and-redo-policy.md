# Satisfaction & Re-do Policy

> **Not legal advice.** This is a plain-language starting template for solo home-service businesses. Contract, consumer-protection, and liability laws vary by state and city. **Have a licensed attorney in your state review and adapt it before you use it.**

A clear re-do policy makes clients trust you more — and protects you from endless callbacks and "refund me" demands after the work is done.

---

## Client-facing policy

**Our promise:** If something we did doesn't match what we agreed to, we'll make it right.

1. **Tell us within [24] hours** ([48] hours for repairs/exterior work) — text a photo to [phone] if you can.
2. **We'll come back** within [2–3] business days to correct the specific area or item at no charge.
3. **Re-dos cover the original scope** — items that were on the estimate and weren't done or weren't done to standard.
4. **Refunds:** re-doing the work is our primary remedy. If we can't correct it after a return visit, we'll discuss a partial refund for the affected portion. [Adjust; note any legal requirements.]

**Not covered:**
- Items that weren't in the original scope
- Issues reported after the reporting window
- Conditions caused after we left (new spills, weather, pets, use)
- Results limited by pre-existing conditions noted before work (set-in stains, oxidation, wear)
- Areas we couldn't access or that the client asked us to skip

## Internal rules (don't publish)

- **Respond within 4 business hours.** Speed defuses most complaints.
- **Look before you argue** — ask for photos; compare with your before/after photos.
- **Fix first, discuss later.** A 30-minute re-do costs less than a bad review.
- **Log every re-do** (date, client, issue, cause). Three re-dos on the same task type = a checklist or training problem.
- **One free re-do per issue.** If a second complaint about the same item isn't valid, explain calmly with photos.
- **Serial complainers:** if a client requests re-dos on most visits, it's time for a direct conversation or ending the relationship (see the Client Retention Text Pack for scripts).

## Scripts

**Acknowledge**
> Thanks for letting me know, [Name] — I'm sorry that wasn't right. Could you send a photo? I'll get it fixed.

**Schedule the re-do**
> I can come back [day/time] to take care of [item] at no charge. Does that work?

**Outside the policy (kindly)**
> Thanks for the photo. That area wasn't part of today's scope, but I'd be glad to add it — it'd be $[__]. Want me to schedule it?

**Pre-existing condition**
> I looked at the before photos from our walk-through — that [stain / scratch] was there before we started, which is why I noted it. It did lighten, but it's set in. I'm happy to try a specialty treatment for $[__] if you'd like.

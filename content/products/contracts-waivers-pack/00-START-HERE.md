# Benchline Contracts & Waivers Pack — Start Here

> **Not legal advice.** This is a plain-language starting template for solo home-service businesses. Contract, consumer-protection, and liability laws vary by state and city. **Have a licensed attorney in your state review and adapt it before you use it.**

Plain-language templates for the paperwork that prevents most home-service disputes. Works for cleaning, handyman, lawn care, pressure washing, detailing, and similar trades.

## How to use this pack

1. **Pick the agreement** (01 or 02) and the policies you need.
2. **Fill every [bracket].** Delete sections that don't apply to your trade.
3. **Take it to a local attorney.** Ask specifically about: deposit limits, cancellation/cooling-off rights for home services, late fee limits, automatic renewal rules, and whether releases are enforceable in your state. A review of short templates is far cheaper than drafting from scratch.
4. **Make it easy to accept.** Most clients will "sign" by replying "Approved" to a text/email or clicking accept in your invoicing app — ask your attorney whether that's sufficient in your state and keep the records.
5. **Use them every time.** Paperwork only protects you if it's consistent.

## A note on insurance

Agreements set expectations; **insurance pays claims.** Carry general liability insurance appropriate for your trade (and garage keepers coverage for detailing, commercial auto if required). Ask your agent what's excluded.

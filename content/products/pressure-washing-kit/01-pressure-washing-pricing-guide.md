# Pressure Washing Pricing Guide

Price by the **measured area**, adjust for **condition**, check against **your hourly target**. That's the whole system — and it keeps quotes consistent whether you quote from the driveway or from a satellite image.

---

## 1. Your hourly target

In **Your Numbers**: with $60k take-home, $20k costs (machine, surface cleaner, trailer/tank, chemicals, insurance), 40 working weeks, and 25 billable hours per week, the target is about **$115/hour**. Pressure washing is seasonal and weather-dependent, and setup/breakdown eats time — so the hourly target is higher than other trades.

## 2. Measure everything

| Surface | How to measure |
|---------|----------------|
| Driveways, patios, walks | Length × width (satellite measuring tool is fine for estimates; confirm on site) |
| House siding | Perimeter × wall height, minus large glass areas. Rough shortcut: **home sq ft × 1.3–1.5 per story** |
| Decks | Floor sq ft (railings/stairs add 20–40%) |
| Fences | Linear feet × number of sides |
| Roofs | Roof sq ft (not house footprint) — pitch adds area |
| Gutters | Linear feet |

## 3. Starting rates (see `10-surface-pricing-matrix.md` for the full matrix)

| Surface | Low | Target | Unit |
|---------|-----|--------|------|
| Concrete driveway / walk | $0.15 | $0.25 | sq ft |
| House soft wash — vinyl | $0.12 | $0.20 | sq ft of wall |
| Wood deck clean + brighten | $0.40 | $0.75 | sq ft |
| Fence (one side) | $1.00 | $2.00 | linear ft |
| Rust / oil stain treatment | $25 | $75 | each |

**Condition multipliers:** Light × 1.0 · Moderate × 1.2 · Heavy × 1.4. These increase price *and* estimated time.

## 4. The minimum job

Default **$175**. Every job costs you loading, driving, water hookup, setup, breakdown, and cleanup before you clean a single square foot. A $90 sidewalk is a money-losing job.

## 5. Check the effective hourly

The Quote Builder estimates hours from your production rates (+0.5 hr setup/breakdown), subtracts chemical and fuel cost, and shows **effective hourly after chemicals**. If it's below target, the Status cell says so.

Default example: 700 sq ft driveway (moderate) + 2,600 sq ft vinyl house wash (light) + 1 oil stain → **$835**, ~4.2 hours, ~$188/hour after chemicals.

## 6. Bundles and packages

Package pricing is how you raise average ticket without discounting your per-surface rates:

| Package | Contents | Pricing approach |
|---------|----------|------------------|
| **Curb appeal** | Driveway + front walk + front porch | Sum of surfaces − 5–10% |
| **Whole home** | House wash + all flatwork + gutter faces | Sum − 10% |
| **Annual plan** | House wash + driveway once a year, booked same month | Lock price for the year; auto-reminder |

Keep a minimum on each package and never discount the minimum.

## 7. What changes the price (ask or look for these)

- **Oil, rust, irrigation (well water) stains** → per-stain treatment, never guaranteed removal
- **Oxidized siding** (chalky residue on your finger) → cleaning can make oxidation more visible; disclose and get the waiver signed
- **Failing paint, loose mortar, old caulk** → soft wash only, disclosed in waiver
- **No outside water spigot / low flow** → bring water (surcharge) or reschedule
- **Two-story or steep grade** → more ladder/extension time
- **Heavy landscaping near walls** → more pre-wetting and rinsing time

## 8. Commercial work

Price commercial flatwork (storefronts, dumpster pads, drive-thrus) hourly or per sq ft with a higher minimum, after checking wastewater recovery rules. It's often recurring (monthly/quarterly) — good off-season revenue.

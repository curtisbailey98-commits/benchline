## Method notes

- **Soft wash** uses low pressure and cleaning solutions for siding, roofs, and painted surfaces. High pressure is used only on concrete and other surfaces rated for it.
- Plants near wash areas are pre-wetted, rinsed after, and covered where needed.
- We need an outdoor spigot with good flow. If water isn't available, a $[__] water surcharge applies.

## Results you can expect (please read)

- **Stains** (oil, rust, fertilizer, irrigation, tire marks, paint) usually lighten but may not fully come out. Stain treatment is priced per stain and is **not a guarantee of removal**.
- **Oxidized siding** (chalky film) may look blotchy or faded after cleaning because the chalk is removed. Cleaning doesn't restore oxidized finishes.
- **Concrete** may show "clean lines" or light/dark areas from previous patchy cleaning, sealers, or age.
- **Wood** may raise grain or look lighter after cleaning and brightening; sealing is a separate service.
- Existing damage (loose paint, cracked or loose mortar, failing caulk, broken seals on windows) may be revealed or worsened. We note these in the pre-job walk-around and waiver.

## Client preparation

- Close all windows and doors; check that exterior outlets have covers.
- Move vehicles, furniture, grills, planters, and fragile décor from work areas (or add moving to the estimate).
- Keep pets and people indoors or away from the work area.
- Tell us about sprinkler systems, outdoor electrical, loose siding, known leaks.

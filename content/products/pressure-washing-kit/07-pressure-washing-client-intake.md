# Pressure Washing Client Intake Questions

---

## Contact
1. Name, mobile, email, property address
2. Owner or renter? (Renters need owner approval)
3. How did you find us?

## What to clean
4. Which surfaces? Driveway · walks · patio · pool deck · house · deck · fence · roof · gutters · other
5. Approximate sizes (we'll measure from satellite and confirm on site)
6. House: siding material (vinyl, fiber cement, stucco, brick, wood)? Stories? Walk-out basement / grade changes?
7. Deck/fence: wood, composite, or vinyl? Stained/sealed? When?
8. Photos of each surface, including the worst areas and any stains

## Condition & history
9. When was it last cleaned? How (pressure washed / soft washed / DIY)?
10. Stains: oil, rust (orange), fertilizer, irrigation (well water), tire marks, paint?
11. Any known issues: loose siding, failing paint, cracked concrete, loose mortar, leaky windows/doors, recent repairs?
12. Is the concrete or pavers sealed?

## Site
13. Outdoor spigot location(s)? Good water pressure? Well water?
14. Outdoor outlets, lights, cameras near wash areas?
15. Landscaping close to walls? Any prized or delicate plants? Fish pond?
16. Pets? Kids? Will anyone need to use the driveway during the job?
17. HOA restrictions on work days/hours?

## Goals
18. Is this for a special event, listing the home, or routine maintenance?
19. Interested in sealing afterward (concrete, pavers, wood)?
20. Want a yearly reminder / maintenance plan?

## Close

> "Thanks! I'll measure from the satellite view and send a written estimate today. On the day, I'll do a quick walk-around with you to note anything that's already worn or damaged — it protects both of us. Sound good?"

**Walk the site before quoting when:** roofs, two-story homes on slopes, heavily oxidized or painted siding, commercial properties, or anything the client describes as "falling apart."

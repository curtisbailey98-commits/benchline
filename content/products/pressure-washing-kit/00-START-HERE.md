# Benchline Pressure Washing Trade Kit — Start Here

This kit applies the Benchline system to exterior cleaning: consistent per-surface pricing, a walk-around and waiver that prevent the classic disputes, and seasonal offers.

## Set it up in one afternoon (about 3 hours)

| Step | Time | File | What to do |
|------|------|------|------------|
| 1 | 20 min | `02-pressure-washing-pricing-calculator.xlsx` → **Your Numbers** | Enter pay goal, costs, season weeks, billable hours |
| 2 | 30 min | **Surface Matrix** sheet + `10-surface-pricing-matrix.md` | Set your per-sq-ft / per-linear-ft rates and your real production rates |
| 3 | 20 min | **Quote Builder** sheet | Run 3 past jobs through it — would they have hit your target hourly? |
| 4 | 30 min | `11-pre-job-damage-waiver.md` + `05-...agreement.md` | Fill in; have a local attorney review. Use the waiver on every job. |
| 5 | 20 min | `07-...intake.md` + `08-...text-scripts.md` | Intake form; save texts |
| 6 | 20 min | `06-...checklists.md` | Print; keep in the truck |
| 7 | 20 min | `09-...calendar.md` | Schedule this quarter's promotions |

## Before you take paid work

- **Insurance:** general liability that covers pressure washing (some policies exclude it or roof work). Ask specifically.
- **Water runoff:** many cities regulate wash water entering storm drains, especially for commercial work and degreasers. Look up your local rules.
- **Chemicals:** follow product labels and Safety Data Sheets. Protect plants, people, pets, and yourself.
- This kit is not legal, insurance, or environmental compliance advice.

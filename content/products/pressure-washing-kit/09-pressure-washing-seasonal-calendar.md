# Pressure Washing Seasonal Upsell & Marketing Calendar

Adjust for your climate — in freezing regions, the season runs roughly March–November; in warm regions, year-round with a summer peak.

| Month | Demand | Offer | Marketing action |
|-------|--------|-------|------------------|
| **Jan** | Low (cold regions) | Commercial flatwork, dumpster pads, gas stations (recurring) | Pitch 5 local businesses a monthly/quarterly flatwork plan |
| **Feb** | Pre-season | Early-bird booking for spring (book now, schedule March/April) | Email/text last year's clients; update Google Business Profile photos |
| **Mar** | Spring starts | House soft wash (pollen, winter grime, algae on north sides) | Before/after posts; door hangers around last year's jobs |
| **Apr** | Pollen peak, curb appeal | Curb appeal package (driveway + walk + porch) | Real estate agents: pre-listing packages |
| **May** | Outdoor living | Deck & patio cleaning, furniture, pool decks | Neighborhood groups; "Memorial Day ready" deadline |
| **Jun** | Peak | Whole-home package; paver clean + sand + seal | Referral push; raise prices if booked 3+ weeks out |
| **Jul** | Peak / heat | Early-morning jobs; fence and deck cleaning before staining | HOA pitch: community-wide discount for 5+ homes on one street |
| **Aug** | Busy | Back-to-school driveway/walks; commercial storefronts | Ask happy clients for reviews with photos |
| **Sep** | Strong | House wash before fall; sealing season (dry weather) | Book fall jobs; offer annual plan |
| **Oct** | Pre-holiday | "Holiday hosting" package: driveway, walks, entry, porch | Promote before holiday décor goes up |
| **Nov** | Slowing | Gutter faces, holiday light install (if insured) | Thank-you texts; annual plan renewals |
| **Dec** | Low | Gift certificates; commercial recurring | Plan next season: price changes, equipment maintenance |

## Recurring revenue ideas
- **Annual residential plan:** house wash + driveway every spring at a locked price.
- **Commercial recurring:** monthly dumpster pads, quarterly storefront sidewalks, drive-thru lanes (check wastewater rules).
- **HOA / neighborhood days:** discounted per-home price for multiple homes on the same day — dense, efficient, great for reviews.

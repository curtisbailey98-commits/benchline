> These are starting points — markets vary widely. Replace with your local prices, and replace **units per hour** with your own measured production after 10–15 jobs.

## Condition multipliers

| Condition | Multiplier | Looks like |
|-----------|-----------|------------|
| **Light** | × 1.0 | Cleaned in the last 1–2 years; light dirt, little organic growth |
| **Moderate** | × 1.2 | Visible green/black growth, dark concrete, pollen buildup |
| **Heavy** | × 1.4 | Never cleaned, thick algae/mildew, heavy tree cover, black streaks |

Multipliers apply to both price and time. A heavy driveway takes longer because of more pre-treatment, slower surface-cleaner passes, and post-treatment.

## Package discounts (optional)

- 2 surfaces: 0–5% off the combined price
- 3+ surfaces or whole-home: up to 10% off
- Never discount below your minimum job price, and never discount stain treatments.

## When to price hourly instead

- Graffiti removal, paint overspray, heavy equipment/fleet washing, restaurant grease, and anything where the result depends on trial-and-error. Quote your hourly target with a minimum and a not-to-exceed estimate.

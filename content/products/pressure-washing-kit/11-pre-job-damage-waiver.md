## 2. What the client understands (initial each)

____ **Stains:** Stain treatment improves appearance but may not fully remove oil, rust, fertilizer, irrigation, paint, or tannin stains.

____ **Oxidation:** Cleaning oxidized siding removes chalky residue and can make fading or unevenness more visible. It doesn't restore the finish.

____ **Paint and coatings:** Loose or failing paint, stain, or sealer may come off during cleaning, even with low pressure.

____ **Water intrusion:** Water may enter through pre-existing gaps, failed seals, open or poorly sealed windows/doors, vents, or damaged siding.

____ **Surfaces:** Concrete may show color variation; wood may raise grain or lighten; pavers may lose joint sand.

____ **Plants:** We'll pre-wet, rinse, and protect plants where practical; mild temporary discoloration can occur, especially on plants already stressed.

____ **Items left in place:** Items the client chose not to move (vehicles, furniture, décor) may get wet or receive overspray; we'll take reasonable care.

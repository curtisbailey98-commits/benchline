# Pressure Washing Job Checklists

---

## A. Equipment check (before leaving)

**Machine & water**
- [ ] Pressure washer: engine oil, pump oil, fuel, unloader working, no leaks
- [ ] Hoses: pressure hose and garden hose — no bulges, cuts, or leaking fittings; O-rings spare
- [ ] Surface cleaner: swivel spins freely, nozzles clear, skirt intact
- [ ] Wand, extension wand, nozzle set (0°/15°/25°/40°/soap) — **no 0° near people, siding, or wood**
- [ ] Downstream injector / soap nozzle or 12V soft-wash pump working
- [ ] Water tank (if used): full, baffled, strapped; buffer tank connections
- [ ] Hose reels, quick-connects, filter on water inlet

**Chemicals (labeled, capped, secured)**
- [ ] House wash solution / sodium hypochlorite (per label), surfactant
- [ ] Degreaser (flatwork), rust remover (if stain treatment booked)
- [ ] Wood cleaner + brightener (decks/fences)
- [ ] SDS sheets on phone or in truck
- [ ] Pump sprayer(s) for pre-treating and plant rinse

**Protection & safety**
- [ ] Safety glasses/goggles, chemical-resistant gloves, boots, hearing protection
- [ ] Plastic sheeting, painter's tape (outlets, lights, cameras), tarps for plants
- [ ] Ladder (and stabilizer) for gutter faces
- [ ] Cones or signs for sidewalks

**Admin**
- [ ] Printed or digital **Pre-Job Waiver** (file 11)
- [ ] Measuring wheel, camera/phone charged, card reader

## B. On arrival

- [ ] Confirm scope with client and surfaces on the estimate
- [ ] **Walk-around with the client** — fill in and sign the Pre-Job Condition Acknowledgment & Waiver
- [ ] Photo every side of the house and every surface **before** you start (include close-ups of existing damage, oxidation, stains)
- [ ] Test spigot flow (fill a 5-gallon bucket; time it). Too low → use tank or reschedule
- [ ] Windows and doors closed; vehicles moved; pets inside
- [ ] Tape exterior outlets, light fixtures, doorbell cameras, and any open vents

## C. Plant & property protection

- [ ] Pre-wet all plants, grass, and shrubs near the wash zone
- [ ] Cover delicate plants and ponds; move potted plants
- [ ] Keep plants wet throughout; rinse thoroughly after
- [ ] Watch runoff — keep it out of storm drains where required

## D. Cleaning order

**House wash:** pre-wet plants → apply solution bottom to top → dwell (don't let it dry) → rinse top to bottom → rinse plants.
**Flatwork:** sweep/blow debris → pre-treat (degreaser, stains) → surface cleaner in overlapping passes → edges with wand → post-treat (if needed) → rinse to the street/lawn per local rules.
**Wood:** low pressure, wide fan (25°–40°), with the grain; cleaner → rinse → brightener → rinse.

## E. Closeout

- [ ] Walk every cleaned area with the client; point out stains that lightened but didn't fully lift (as disclosed)
- [ ] **After photos** from the same angles as before photos
- [ ] Remove tape and plastic; return moved items
- [ ] Final plant rinse
- [ ] Coil hoses; check for anything left on site
- [ ] Take payment; mention annual reminder / maintenance plan

## F. After

- [ ] Day-of text (file 08) + review request after positive reply
- [ ] Log job: actual hours vs. estimate per surface → update production rates in the Surface Matrix
- [ ] Rinse equipment, flush chemical lines with fresh water
- [ ] Restock chemicals; note any equipment repairs

# Pressure Washing Text Scripts

---

## Before the job

**Estimate sent**
> Hi [Name], here's your estimate for [surfaces]: [link]. Total $[__]. I've included notes on what to expect with the [oil stains / oxidation]. Reply "Approved" and I'll get you on the schedule.

**Estimate follow-up (3 days)**
> Hi [Name], checking in on the estimate. If you want to start with just the [driveway], I can do that too. Want me to hold [day]?

**Day-before prep**
> Hi [Name], see you tomorrow between [window]! Please close all windows, move cars off the driveway, and bring in pets. I'll need the outdoor spigot at [location]. I'll do a quick walk-around with you when I arrive.

**Weather reschedule**
> Hi [Name], [rain / high wind / freezing temps] tomorrow means I can't do a quality job. Let's move to [option 1] or [option 2] — which works?

**On my way**
> On my way — about [__] minutes out.

## After the job

**Day-of**
> Thanks, [Name]! Your [driveway / house] is done — before and after photos attached. The [oil stain] lightened a lot but didn't fully lift, as we discussed. Plants got a good rinse. Any questions, just text me.

**Review request (after positive reply)**
> So glad you like it! A quick Google review with a photo helps neighbors see the difference: [link]. Thank you!

**Neighbor offer (same week, only if you actually have openings)**
> Hi [Name], I'm back in your neighborhood on [date] and have room for two more jobs. If a neighbor mentions your driveway, feel free to share my number — I'll waive the minimum for them since I'm already nearby.

**Annual reminder (11 months later)**
> Hi [Name], it's been about a year since we washed your [house / driveway]. Want me to put you on the [month] schedule at last year's price?

## Upsells

**Sealing (after flatwork)**
> Now that your pavers are clean, it's the best time to seal them — it helps keep stains and weeds out. For your [___ sq ft], sealing is $[__]. Want me to add it next visit?

**Whole-home package**
> Since I'll already be set up for the driveway, adding the house wash and gutter faces would be $[__] total instead of $[__] separately. Want the full package?

## Problems

**Client worried about a result**
> Thanks for letting me know. Can you send a photo? I'll come take a look [day/time]. If it's something I missed, I'll re-clean it at no charge.

**Damage concern**
> I'm sorry to hear that. Please send photos and I'll come by [day/time] to look. I have before photos from the walk-around, so we can compare and figure out the right next step together.

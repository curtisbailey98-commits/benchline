# Pressure Washing Service Agreement

> **Not legal advice.** A plain-language template for solo exterior cleaning businesses. Contract, consumer protection, and environmental (wastewater) rules differ by state and city. **Have a licensed attorney in your state review and adapt it before use.** Use together with `11-pre-job-damage-waiver.md`.

---

**Business:** [Business name] · [address] · [phone] · [email]
**Client:** [Name] · [property address] · [phone] · [email]
**Estimate #:** [___] (attached)

## 1. Services
We'll clean the surfaces listed in the estimate using the methods described. Additional surfaces or services require approval (text/email is fine) and may change the price.

## 2. Scheduling and weather
- Scheduled date: [___]. We don't work in rain, high winds, or freezing temperatures. Weather rescheduling is free for both of us.
- Client cancellations with less than [24/48] hours' notice may be charged $[___].

## 3. Price and payment
- Price per the estimate: $[___]. Payment due on completion.
- Late payments over [7/14] days incur [a fee of $__ / as permitted by law].

## 4. Client responsibilities
- Provide access to an outdoor water spigot with adequate flow and pressure, and electricity if needed.
- Close windows and doors; move vehicles and movable items; secure pets.
- Disclose known issues: loose siding or trim, failing paint, leaking windows or doors, cracked foundations, outdoor electrical, fragile plants, recent sealing/painting, and sprinkler systems.

## 5. Results
- We'll perform the work with reasonable care and industry-standard methods.
- We don't guarantee removal of stains (rust, oil, fertilizer, irrigation, paint, tannin), oxidation, efflorescence, or organic staining embedded in porous surfaces.
- Surfaces may look different after cleaning (e.g., oxidation more visible, concrete color variation, raised wood grain).

## 6. Pre-existing conditions and waiver
- Before starting, we'll walk the property with you and record existing conditions on the **Pre-Job Condition Acknowledgment & Waiver**.
- We aren't responsible for damage that results from pre-existing conditions noted there or that weren't reasonably visible (e.g., failing window seals, loose mortar, deteriorated paint, improperly installed siding).

## 7. Plants and property
- We pre-wet, rinse, and cover plants near wash areas where practical. Mild temporary discoloration of plants is possible; we're not responsible for plants already stressed or diseased.
- Water may enter through failed seals, open windows, or gaps in doors, windows, siding, or outlets; we're not responsible for water intrusion through pre-existing defects.

## 8. Damage and insurance
- We carry general liability insurance [carrier; certificate on request].
- Report any damage you believe we caused within [48] hours with photos. We'll inspect and, if caused by our negligence, repair or reimburse reasonable costs.

## 9. Satisfaction
If an area we cleaned wasn't completed as described, tell us within [48] hours. We'll return to re-clean that area at no charge. Re-cleaning is our primary remedy.

## 10. Water runoff
We follow applicable local rules for wash water. [Describe your practice: e.g., "For residential jobs, we use biodegradable products where possible and avoid discharging degreasers into storm drains."]

## 11. Photos
We take before/after photos for our records. We'll only share them publicly with your permission.

## 12. General
This agreement, the estimate, and the signed waiver are the whole agreement. [State] law governs.

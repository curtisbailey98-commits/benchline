## Example — Lawn care: trimming and edging a standard residential yard

**Time target:** 10 minutes for a 6,000 sq ft lot

**Why it matters:** clean edges are what clients notice most from the street.

**Tools:** string trimmer (line loaded), stick edger, safety glasses, hearing protection.

**Safety:** eye and ear protection on; check for people, pets, cars, and windows before trimming; never trim toward a person or vehicle.

| # | Step | Standard |
|---|------|----------|
| 1 | Walk the yard; remove debris; note sprinkler heads | Nothing in the path that could be thrown |
| 2 | Edge driveway, walks, and curb | Straight vertical line; no grass over concrete |
| 3 | Trim around obstacles (trees, beds, fences, mailbox) | Matches mowed height; no scalping; **no line touching tree bark** |
| 4 | Trim fence lines and hard-to-mow areas | No tall strips left |
| 5 | Hand debris to the blower operator / blow clean | No clippings left on hard surfaces |

**Common mistakes:** scalping near beds, hitting tree trunks, trimming too high, blowing clippings into the street or storm drain.

## How to write SOPs fast
1. Do the task yourself and **record a voice memo** narrating each step.
2. Transcribe into the table (or let your phone transcribe it).
3. Take one photo of the finished standard.
4. Have the helper follow it once and mark where they got confused. Fix those lines.

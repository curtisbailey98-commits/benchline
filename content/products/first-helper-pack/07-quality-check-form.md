## Trade-specific areas to score (examples)
- **Cleaning:** kitchen counters/sink, bathroom toilet/tub, mirrors (no streaks), floors (edges), dusting (high & low)
- **Lawn:** mowing stripes/height, trim lines, edges, blow-off, gate latched
- **Pressure washing:** even surface-cleaner passes (no stripes), edges, plant rinse, runoff handling
- **Detailing:** vacuum completeness (under seats), glass (no streaks), door jambs, wheels/tires, towel marks on paint
- **Handyman:** level/plumb, fasteners into studs where required, caulk lines, cleanup

# Job Post Templates

Short, specific, honest. Say the pay range, the schedule, and what the day actually looks like. Check your state's pay-transparency and anti-discrimination rules; don't ask for age, family status, or other protected information.

---

## General template

**Part-Time [Trade] Helper — $[__]–$[__]/hour — [Town/Area]**

[Business] is a small, owner-operated [trade] business serving [area]. I'm looking for a dependable helper to work alongside me [#] days a week.

**What you'll do**
- [3–5 concrete tasks]
- Work as a two-person team with me (the owner) on residential jobs
- Keep equipment clean and organized

**Schedule**
- [Days], about [start]–[end] ([#] hours/week)
- [Seasonal: March–November / year-round]

**Pay**
- $[__]–$[__]/hour depending on experience, paid [weekly/biweekly]
- [Mileage/drive-time policy] · [Gear provided]

**You're a fit if you**
- Show up on time, every time — this matters more than experience
- Take pride in doing careful work in people's homes/properties
- Can [physical requirement relevant to the job, e.g., lift 40 lbs, work outdoors in heat]
- Have reliable transportation to [meeting point] [and a valid driver's license, if you'll drive]

**To apply:** text [phone] or email [email] with your name, a sentence about why you're interested, and the days you're available. No résumé needed.

---

## Trade-specific task lists (swap into "What you'll do")

**Cleaning:** clean kitchens and bathrooms to a written checklist; vacuum, mop, dust; work in clients' homes respectfully; handle cleaning products safely.

**Lawn care:** operate mowers, string trimmers, edgers, and blowers (training provided); load and unload a trailer; bed cleanups and mulch; work outdoors in varied weather.

**Pressure washing:** set up hoses and equipment; pre-wet and protect plants; operate a surface cleaner under supervision; clean up and load equipment.

**Detailing:** vacuum and clean interiors; hand-wash and dry vehicles; clean wheels and tires; follow product and safety instructions.

**Handyman:** carry and stage materials; assist with installs, assembly, and repairs; protect floors and clean up; hold, measure, and cut under supervision.

## Where to post
- Your own social pages and neighborhood groups (your clients know people)
- Local job boards and community college/trade school boards
- Ask current clients and other local operators for referrals
- A yard sign or truck sign: "Hiring helpers — text [phone]"

## Screening text (reply to applicants)
> Thanks for your interest, [Name]! A few quick questions: 1) Which days/times are you available? 2) Do you have reliable transportation to [area] by [time]? 3) Any experience with [trade] (none is fine)? 4) When could you start?

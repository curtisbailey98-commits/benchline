# Interview Questions, Reference Check & Paid Trial Day

For a helper role, **reliability and attitude** matter more than experience. You can teach technique; you can't teach showing up.

---

## Phone screen (10 minutes)

1. What interested you in this job?
2. Walk me through your availability — any days or times you can't work?
3. How would you get to [meeting point] by [time]? What's your backup if that falls through?
4. Tell me about a job where you had to be on time every day. How did it go?
5. This job is physical — [describe: on your feet all day, outdoors in heat, lifting]. Any concerns?
6. What questions do you have for me?

✅ Move forward if: clear availability, reliable transport plan, honest about the physical work.

## In-person interview (30 minutes, at a coffee shop or job site)

**Reliability**
- Tell me about the last time you were going to be late for work. What did you do?
- What would a past boss say is your best quality? Your worst?

**Work quality**
- Tell me about something you did that you were proud of because it was done right.
- If you finished a task and noticed you'd missed something, but I hadn't noticed — what would you do?

**Customers & trust**
- You'll be working in people's homes/yards/cars. What does being respectful of someone's property look like to you?
- A customer asks you a question you can't answer. What do you do?

**Coachability**
- Tell me about a time someone corrected how you did something. How did you handle it?
- How do you prefer to learn — being shown, reading a checklist, or trying and getting feedback?

**Situational**
- It's 3:30, we have one more job, and you're tired. How do you handle that?
- You break something at a client's house. What do you do?

**Don't ask** about age, marital/family status, pregnancy, religion, national origin, disability, or other protected characteristics. Ask only whether they can perform the essential job duties (with or without reasonable accommodation).

## Reference check script (5 minutes)
> "Hi, I'm [name] with [Business]. [Candidate] applied to work with me and listed you as a reference. Do you have a few minutes?"

1. How do you know [Candidate], and for how long?
2. How reliable were they about showing up on time?
3. How did they handle feedback?
4. Would you hire or work with them again? *(Listen for hesitation.)*

## Paid trial day

A trial day lets both of you see if it works. **Pay for it** — trial work is work, and in most cases must be paid at least minimum wage. Set up payroll (or talk to your accountant about the right way to pay a trial day) before it happens.

**Plan (4–6 hours)**
1. **First 30 min:** tour of the truck/equipment, safety basics, how we treat clients' property.
2. **Job 1:** they shadow and do simple tasks (loading, prep, basic cleaning/trimming).
3. **Job 2:** they do a task from a written checklist while you work nearby.
4. **Last 15 min:** ask them what was hardest; give one piece of praise and one correction and watch how they take it.

**Watch for:** on time? phone away? asks good questions? follows the checklist? careful with property? energy at the end of the day?

**Decide within 24 hours.** Text either way:
> "Thanks for today, [Name]. I'd like to offer you the position at $[__]/hour starting [date]. Can you confirm?"
> "Thanks for coming out today, [Name]. I've decided to go another direction, but I appreciate your time — you'll be paid for today by [date]."

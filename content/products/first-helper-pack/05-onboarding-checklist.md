# First-Week Onboarding Checklist

---

## Before day one (you)
- [ ] Offer confirmed in writing: pay rate, schedule, start date, pay day, who to call if sick
- [ ] Payroll set up; new-hire paperwork ready (e.g., Form I-9, Form W-4, state forms)
- [ ] Workers' comp and insurance updated
- [ ] Gear ready: shirts with your logo (optional), PPE in their size (gloves, eye/ear protection, boots guidance)
- [ ] Three SOPs printed (see `06-training-sop-template.md`)
- [ ] Your schedule for week one has **easier jobs and extra time** built in

## Day one
- [ ] Paperwork completed and copies filed
- [ ] Walk through: how we treat clients, homes, and property (shoe covers, no personal phone use in homes, no photos without permission, how to answer client questions: "Let me check with [owner]")
- [ ] Safety: chemical handling and labels, equipment safety, lifting, heat/cold, what to do if hurt
- [ ] Truck/equipment tour: where things live, how to load and unload
- [ ] Shadow the first job; do simple tasks on the second
- [ ] End-of-day: what went well, one thing to work on, questions

## Days two to five
- [ ] Day 2: one SOP task done independently, you check it
- [ ] Day 3: two SOP tasks independently; introduce the quality check form
- [ ] Day 4: full job as a team with them leading one area
- [ ] Day 5: review the week — pay, hours, what's next; ask what would make the job better

## Communication norms (write these down and share)
- Running late or sick: **call** (don't just text) at least [2] hours before start
- Client complaints or damage: tell the owner immediately; never argue with a client
- Phones: stay in pocket at clients' properties except for work tasks
- Breaks and lunch: [policy per your state's rules]
- Pay day: [day]; timesheet method: [app / paper]

## 30-day check-in
- [ ] Pay rate review (raise at 30/60/90 days if promised)
- [ ] Quality scores trend (from quality check forms)
- [ ] Can they handle any job solo yet? Which ones?
- [ ] What do they want to learn next?

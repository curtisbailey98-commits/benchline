# Seasonal Lawn Maintenance Contract

> **Not legal advice.** A plain-language template for a seasonal (prepaid or monthly-billed) lawn maintenance plan. Rules for automatic renewals, prepayments, and cancellations vary by state. **Have a licensed attorney in your state review and adapt it before use.**

---

**Business:** [Business name] · [address] · [phone] · [email]
**Client:** [Name] · [property address] · [phone] · [email]
**Season:** [Start date] through [End date] ("Season")

## 1. Included services

| Service | Frequency / timing | Included |
|---------|-------------------|----------|
| Mowing, trimming, edging, blowing | Weekly, approx. [##] visits in the Season | ✓ |
| Spring cleanup | Once, [Mar/Apr] | ☐ |
| Mulch installation — [#] cubic yards | Once, [Apr/May] | ☐ |
| Bed edging (redefine) | Once, spring | ☐ |
| Shrub / hedge trimming | [#] times | ☐ |
| Core aeration + overseeding | Once, [month] | ☐ |
| Fall leaf cleanup | [#] visits, [Oct/Nov] | ☐ |

Visit counts are estimates. Mowing frequency follows grass growth — we may skip visits during drought or dormancy and add visits during heavy growth. **The Season price doesn't change for normal weather variation** as long as we provide at least [##] mowing visits. If we provide fewer than [##] because of our scheduling, we'll credit $[__] per missed visit.

## 2. Season price and payment

- **Season total:** $[_____]
- Payment option (choose one):
  - ☐ **Monthly:** $[___] per month × [#] months, charged on the [1st] from [month] to [month] to the card on file
  - ☐ **Prepaid in full:** $[_____] (includes a [5]% prepay discount), due by [date]
- Services outside this plan are quoted and billed separately.
- Late payments over [15] days may pause service until paid.

## 3. Weather and scheduling

- Service day: [day]; rain or wet conditions move service to the next suitable day.
- Seasonal services (cleanups, aeration, mulch) are scheduled within the stated windows; we'll text you before we come.

## 4. Property responsibilities

- Clear toys, hoses, furniture, and pet waste from service areas.
- Mark sprinkler heads, invisible fences, lighting, and hidden objects. We're not responsible for damage to unmarked items.
- Provide gate access on service days.

## 5. Damage and insurance

We carry general liability insurance [carrier; certificate on request]. Report damage within [48] hours with photos. We'll repair or reimburse reasonable costs of damage caused by our negligence.

## 6. Cancellation

- **By you:** cancel any time with [14] days' written notice.
  - Monthly plan: payments already made cover services delivered; if services delivered (valued at the per-visit and per-service prices in the estimate) exceed payments made, the difference is due. [Confirm your state's rules on this "true-up".]
  - Prepaid plan: we refund the unused portion, calculated as the prepaid amount minus services delivered at regular (non-discounted) prices.
- **By us:** we may end the plan with [14] days' written notice and refund any prepaid amount for services not delivered.

## 7. Renewal

This plan **does not renew automatically**. We'll send next season's price by [January 31]. Reply to accept and keep your route spot. [If you want auto-renewal, check your state's automatic renewal disclosure laws first.]

# Benchline Lawn Care Trade Kit — Start Here

This kit applies the Benchline system to a solo mowing and maintenance route: price per visit from real production rates, sell seasonal contracts, and keep routes dense.

## Set it up in one afternoon (about 3 hours)

| Step | Time | File | What to do |
|------|------|------|------------|
| 1 | 20 min | `02-lawn-care-pricing-calculator.xlsx` → **Your Numbers** | Enter pay goal, costs, **mowing-season weeks**, billable hours |
| 2 | 30 min | **Mow Pricing** sheet | Time 3 of your own yards; set your mowing rate (sq ft/hr), trim minutes, setup minutes |
| 3 | 20 min | **Seasonal Contract** sheet | Set visits per month for your climate and the services included |
| 4 | 30 min | **Route Planner** sheet + `11-route-planning-guide.md` | Enter current clients by day/zone; see revenue per hour including drive |
| 5 | 20 min | `10-seasonal-maintenance-contract.md` + `05-...agreement.md` | Fill in terms; have a local attorney review |
| 6 | 20 min | `07-...intake.md` + `08-...text-scripts.md` | Build intake form; save rain-delay and follow-up texts |
| 7 | 20 min | `06-...checklists.md` + `09-...calendar.md` | Print checklists; schedule the year's upsells |

## Licensing and chemicals

Many states require a **pesticide/fertilizer applicator license** (and business license/insurance) to apply weed control, insecticides, or even some fertilizers for hire. Check your state department of agriculture before offering applications. This kit is not legal advice.

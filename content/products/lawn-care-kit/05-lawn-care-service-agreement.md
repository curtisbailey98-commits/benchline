# Lawn Care Service Agreement (Per-Visit)

> **Not legal advice.** A plain-language template for solo lawn care operators. Contract, consumer, and pesticide-application rules differ by state. **Have a licensed attorney in your state review and adapt it before use.** For seasonal prepaid or monthly plans, use `10-seasonal-maintenance-contract.md`.

---

**Business:** [Business name] · [address] · [phone] · [email]
**Client:** [Name] · [property address] · [phone] · [email]
**Start date:** [date]

## 1. Services
We'll provide the services in the accepted estimate #[___] at the property. Additional services are quoted and approved separately (text or email approval is fine).

## 2. Schedule and weather
- Service day: [day], weekly / every 2 weeks, [start month] through [end month].
- We don't mow when grass is too wet or during storms; we'll service the next suitable day. During drought, we may skip visits (not charged) or mow less often to protect the lawn — we'll tell you first.

## 3. Price and payment
- Price per visit: $[___]. Additional services per the estimate.
- Payment: [card on file charged after each visit / invoiced monthly, due within [10] days].
- Late invoices over [15] days may pause service until paid; late fee $[__] [as permitted by law].
- Prices may change with **30 days' written notice**.

## 4. Property preparation
- Please remove toys, hoses, furniture, and pet waste from lawn areas before service. We'll skip areas we can't safely mow and aren't responsible for items left in the lawn.
- Mark sprinkler heads, invisible dog fences, low-voltage lighting, and other hidden hazards. We're not responsible for damage to unmarked items in the lawn.
- Keep pets inside during service.

## 5. Access
- You'll provide gate access (codes/locks). If we can't access the property on the service day, the visit may be charged [in full / at $__] as a lockout.

## 6. Damage
- We carry general liability insurance [carrier; certificate on request].
- Report damage you believe we caused within [48] hours with photos. We'll repair or reimburse reasonable costs for damage caused by our negligence.
- We're not responsible for: unmarked hidden objects, pre-existing damage, normal wear from mowing equipment on soft/wet ground, or thrown debris from items left in the lawn despite reasonable care.

## 7. Chemical applications
- [If you don't hold an applicator license:] We do not apply pesticides, herbicides, or regulated fertilizers.
- [If licensed:] Applications are performed under license #[___]. We'll notify you before each application as required by law, including re-entry instructions for people and pets.

## 8. Cancellation
- Either party may end service with **[14] days' written notice**. Completed visits remain payable.

## 9. General
This agreement and the estimate are the whole agreement. [State] law governs.

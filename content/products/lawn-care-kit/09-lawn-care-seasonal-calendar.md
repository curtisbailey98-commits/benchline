# Lawn Care Seasonal Upsell & Marketing Calendar

Timing below fits most of the U.S. with **cool-season grasses** (fescue, bluegrass, rye). For **warm-season grasses** (Bermuda, zoysia, St. Augustine, centipede), shift aeration/overseeding to late spring–early summer and adjust mowing start/end to your climate.

| Month | Field work | Upsell / offer | Marketing action |
|-------|-----------|----------------|------------------|
| **Jan** | Equipment maintenance, snow/holiday-light side work | Seasonal plan renewals with early-renewal perk | Text/email every client the new season's price; lock routes |
| **Feb** | Blade sharpening, mower service | Spring cleanup pre-booking; seasonal plans | Door hangers in your densest zones; update Google Business Profile photos |
| **Mar** | Spring cleanups, first mows (warm areas) | Spring cleanup, bed edging | Ask last year's clients for referrals on their street |
| **Apr** | Mowing starts in most areas | Mulch install, bed edging, shrub shaping | Yard signs (with permission) at clients' homes in new zones |
| **May** | Peak growth | Weekly service upgrade for biweekly clients; mulch | Waitlist for full routes; only add clients inside existing zones |
| **Jun** | Peak growth, heat begins | Hedge trimming; warm-season aeration | Review revenue/hour by day; raise price on outlier yards |
| **Jul** | Heat, drought skips | Irrigation-friendly mowing height; bed weeding | Offer fall cleanup and aeration pre-booking to seasonal clients |
| **Aug** | Growth slows (cool-season) | Book aeration + overseed for Sep | Email/text an aeration explainer with a price for their lawn |
| **Sep** | Cool-season aeration + overseeding window | Aeration, overseeding, shrub trim | Referral push: "Refer a neighbor, get a free cut" |
| **Oct** | Leaves begin | Fall cleanup (multiple visits) | Announce cleanup schedule; sell leaf visits by street |
| **Nov** | Final mows, leaf cleanups | Final cleanup, gutter cleaning (1-story) | Thank-you texts; request reviews from happiest clients |
| **Dec** | Off-season | Holiday lights (if insured), snow removal (if equipped) | Plan price changes; prepare seasonal-plan offers |

## Every season
- Sell new clients in **existing zones first**.
- Re-price yards that consistently run long.
- Keep a list of "next year" upsells noted on each property during the season.

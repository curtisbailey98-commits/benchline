# Lawn Care Client Intake Questions

---

## Contact
1. Name, mobile, email, property address
2. Owner or renter? (Renters: who pays — you or the landlord?)
3. How did you hear about us?

## Property
4. Front, back, and side yards — all included?
5. Gated backyard? Gate width (can a 36"/48" mower fit)? Code or lock?
6. Slopes, ditches, or retention ponds?
7. Obstacles: trees, beds, play sets, trampolines, fences, dog runs?
8. Hidden hazards: sprinkler heads, invisible dog fence, low-voltage lights, septic lids, drain pipes?
9. Grass type if you know it (we'll check on the first visit)
10. Current condition: when was it last mowed? How tall is it now?

> Before quoting, measure turf on a satellite map view. Ask for a photo of the backyard if it's not visible from the street.

## Service
11. Weekly or every 2 weeks? (Recommend weekly in peak growth)
12. Preferred day? (We service by neighborhood — the day depends on the route)
13. Bag clippings or mulch them back into the lawn? (Mulching is better for most lawns and is included; bagging is extra)
14. Interested in: spring cleanup, mulch, bed edging, hedge trimming, aeration/overseeding, fall leaf cleanup?
15. Want a **seasonal plan** with even monthly payments?

## Household
16. Pets? Will they be outside on service days? Is pet waste picked up?
17. Kids' toys usually in the yard?
18. Any HOA rules (mowing height, days/hours, clippings)?
19. Irrigation system? Schedule? (We'll avoid mowing right after watering)

## Payment
20. Card on file charged after each visit, monthly invoice, or seasonal plan?

## Close

> "Thanks! Your property is in our [zone] route, which we service on [day]. I'll send an estimate today. Most clients in your neighborhood are on the seasonal plan — it includes spring and fall cleanups and spreads the cost evenly. Want me to include that option?"

**Decline or charge more:** properties far outside your zones, severe slopes without the right equipment, yards that need a full overgrowth reclaim (quote as a separate job first), or clients who want "just mow when it looks long" with no schedule.

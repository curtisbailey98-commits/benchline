# Lawn Care Text Scripts

---

## Service updates

**Welcome (after booking)**
> Hi [Name], welcome aboard! Your lawn is on our [day] route starting [date]. Please pick up toys and pet waste before service days and let me know about any sprinkler heads or hidden lines. — [Your name], [Business]

**Service complete (optional, same day)**
> Hi [Name], your lawn was serviced today. Gate is latched. Anything look off, just reply here.

**Rain delay**
> Hi [Name], rain's pushing today's route back — we'll be out [tomorrow / next dry day, likely [day]]. Nothing you need to do.

**Drought / heat skip**
> Hi [Name], with the heat and no rain, your grass has barely grown. We're skipping this week's cut to avoid stressing the lawn — no charge. Back on [date].

**Locked gate**
> Hi [Name], I'm at your property but the gate's locked. Front's done. Can you unlock it in the next 15 minutes? Otherwise I'll get the back next visit.

## Follow-up and reviews

**2-week check-in (new client)**
> Hi [Name], how's the lawn looking after the first couple of cuts? Any spots you want us to pay more attention to?

**Review request (after positive feedback)**
> Glad you like it! If you have a minute, a Google review helps neighbors find reliable lawn care: [link]. Thanks!

**Neighbor referral**
> Hi [Name], I've got room for a couple more yards on your street on [day]. If a neighbor needs a mowing service, I'd appreciate you passing my number along — same-street yards help me keep prices steady.

## Seasonal upsells

**Spring (Feb–Mar)**
> Hi [Name], booking spring cleanups now: beds cleared, sticks picked up, edges cut, first mow. $[__] for your yard. Want me to add it before your first regular visit?

**Mulch (Apr)**
> Hi [Name], your beds could use a fresh layer of mulch. [#] yards installed would be about $[__]. Want me to schedule it?

**Aeration + overseed (your season)**
> Hi [Name], it's the right time for aeration and overseeding for your grass type — it's the best thing you can do for thin spots. $[__] for your lawn. Interested?

**Fall cleanup (Oct)**
> Hi [Name], leaves are starting. I'm scheduling fall cleanups for [weeks]. Most yards on your street need 2 visits — $[__] each. Want me to pencil you in?

**Season renewal (Jan–Feb)**
> Hi [Name], thank you for a great season! I'm setting routes for [year]. Your weekly price will be $[__] [same as last year / up $__ due to fuel and equipment costs]. Reply YES to keep your spot on the [day] route.

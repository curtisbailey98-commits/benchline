# Lawn Care Job Checklists

---

## A. Morning truck & trailer check (10 minutes)

**Equipment**
- [ ] Mower(s): oil level, fuel, tire pressure, blades sharp (sharpen every 8–10 mowing hours), deck clear, safety switches work
- [ ] String trimmer: line loaded, spare spool, fuel mixed correctly (2-stroke ratio on the label)
- [ ] Edger: blade condition, fuel
- [ ] Backpack/handheld blower: fuel, filter clean
- [ ] Hedge trimmer (if scheduled)

**Trailer & truck**
- [ ] Hitch pinned, safety chains crossed, lights working
- [ ] Equipment strapped; ramp latched
- [ ] Gas cans full and secured upright, away from the cab
- [ ] Tarps, leaf bags, rake, broom, shovel, hand pruners

**Safety**
- [ ] Safety glasses, hearing protection, gloves, boots
- [ ] Water, sunscreen, first-aid kit
- [ ] Phone charged; route loaded in order

## B. Arrival at each property

- [ ] Park so trailer doesn't block driveways or mailboxes
- [ ] Walk the lawn quickly: toys, hoses, rocks, sticks, pet waste, new hazards (sprinkler heads, low-voltage lighting)
- [ ] Note anything broken **before** you start — photo it
- [ ] Check gate: latch works? Pets inside?

## C. Service order

1. **Trim first** around obstacles, fences, beds, and trees (don't girdle tree trunks — use a trunk guard or keep the line off bark).
2. **Edge** hard surfaces: driveway, sidewalk, curb.
3. **Mow:** perimeter laps first, then stripes. Alternate direction each visit to avoid ruts. Discharge away from beds, cars, windows, and people.
4. **Mowing height:** never remove more than 1/3 of the blade in one cut. Raise height in summer heat/drought.
5. **Blow** clippings off driveways, walks, patios, and the street (don't blow into storm drains).

## D. Before you leave

- [ ] Walk the property once: missed strips, clippings on hard surfaces, scalped spots
- [ ] Gate closed and latched — **double-check**
- [ ] Note upsell opportunities: overgrown shrubs, bare patches, edging needed, leaf load
- [ ] "Service complete" text or app notification (file 08)

## E. End of day

- [ ] Clean mower deck (scrape or wash) — especially after wet cuts
- [ ] Refuel for tomorrow (not in an enclosed space)
- [ ] Log visits completed and any skipped (rain, locked gate) — reschedule skips
- [ ] Update Route Planner if a stop took longer than planned
- [ ] Charge batteries (battery equipment)

## F. Weekly

- [ ] Sharpen or swap mower blades
- [ ] Grease fittings, check belts
- [ ] Clean air filters
- [ ] Review revenue per hour by day (Route Planner)

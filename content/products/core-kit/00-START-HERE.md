# Welcome to Benchline — Your Back Office Starts Here

You're good at the work. This kit handles the business side: what to charge, how to quote, how to follow up, how to run a job without forgetting things, how to get reviews, and what you actually kept at the end of the week.

Everything is a plain file you own: **Markdown** (opens in any text editor; imports into Notion, Google Docs, or Obsidian), **XLSX** spreadsheets with working formulas (Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice, Numbers), and **CSV**. No software to learn, no subscription required.

## Your first week

**Day 1 — Set your prices (45 min)**
1. Open `08-job-pricing-calculator.xlsx` → **Your Numbers**. Enter your pay goal, business costs, and realistic billable hours. Write down your **target hourly rate**.
2. Read `07-job-pricing-guide.md`. Set your minimum job price.
3. Edit `pricing-sheets.csv` (or the Rate Card tab) with your prices. Delete trades you don't do.

**Day 2 — Get your quoting ready (30 min)**
4. Copy `02-estimate-template.md` into Google Docs, Notion, or your invoicing app. Add your logo, business name, and payment methods.
5. Price your next real job with the **Price a Job** tab before you send the estimate.

**Day 3 — Set up follow-up (30 min)**
6. Save the texts from `09-lead-follow-up-system.md` and `04-follow-up-sms.md` as phone shortcuts or canned replies.
7. Follow up on every open quote from the last two weeks (use the Day 3 script).

**Day 4 — Intake and job day (30 min)**
8. Turn `01-intake-script.md` into a booking form (Google Forms works) or keep it by the phone.
9. Print `03-job-checklist.md`. Laminate it. Mark your trade-specific lines in pen.

**Day 5 — Reviews (15 min)**
10. Get your Google review link (Google Business Profile → "Ask for reviews"). Paste it into `05-google-review-ask.md`.
11. Ask your three happiest recent customers.

**End of week — Money (30 min)**
12. Open `10-weekly-money-dashboard.xlsx`, delete the example rows, log this week's jobs and expenses, and look at **What you actually kept** and **Jobs below your target**.
13. Put a recurring 30-minute "money check-in" on your calendar (see `06-weekly-money-review.md`).

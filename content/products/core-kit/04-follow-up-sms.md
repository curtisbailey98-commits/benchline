# Benchline Follow-Up SMS Scripts
## Day-of · 24h · 7-day — short, human, no spam energy

**Rules:** One ask per message. Always give an easy out. Never auto-blast without consent.

---

### Day-of (within 2 hours of finishing)

> Hey [Name] — [Your Name] here. Appreciate you trusting me with [job]. If anything feels off in the next day, text me and I'll make it right. — [Business]

---

### 24-hour check-in

> Morning [Name] — quick check: everything still look good from yesterday? If yes, awesome. If not, tell me what to fix.

**If they reply positively →** move to review ask (script 05).  
**If issue →** schedule fix before any review ask.

---

### 7-day nurture (optional, for recurring trades)

> Hey [Name] — [Your Name]. Hitting the 1-week mark since [job]. Most folks book the next visit around [interval]. Want me to pencil a hold for [suggested window]? No pressure either way.

---

### No-show / reschedule (customer delayed you)

> Hi [Name] — I waited on site until [time]. Happy to rebook; the next open slot is [option]. Reschedule fee is $[X] if under 2 hours notice — fair warning so we're both good.

---

### Estimate follow-up (no reply in 48h)

> [Name] — circling back on the estimate I sent [day]. Still interested, or should I close it out so the slot opens up? Totally fine either way.

---

### Stop language (honor immediately)

If they say stop / unsubscribe:  
> Got it — I won't text again. Thanks for the chance to help.

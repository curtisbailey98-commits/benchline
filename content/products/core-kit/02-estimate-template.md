## Scope of work (customer-facing)

Write what you **will** do in plain language. Example:

1. Arrive in branded / clean vehicle within agreed window  
2. Protect floors / furniture as needed  
3. Complete: [list tasks]  
4. Walkthrough with customer before leaving  
5. Haul debris / reset space  

**Not included:** permits, structural repairs, after-hours fees, materials not listed below.

## Margin guardrails (internal — do not send)

| Rule | Default |
|------|---------|
| Target gross margin | ≥ 45% after materials + travel |
| Minimum job | $X (your floor — never quote below) |
| Rush / same-day | +20–35% |
| Hard access / stairs / pets unmanaged | +10–25% |
| Wait time beyond 15 min | bill in 15-min blocks |

**Quick check:** `(Price − Materials − Travel − Disposal) / Price ≥ 0.45`

If below target: raise price, reduce scope, or decline.

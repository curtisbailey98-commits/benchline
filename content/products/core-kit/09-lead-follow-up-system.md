# Lead-to-Customer Follow-Up System
## For people who asked for a price but haven't booked

Most quotes aren't rejected — they're forgotten. A short, polite follow-up sequence recovers a meaningful share of them. This file covers the **before-booking** stage; `04-follow-up-sms.md` covers after-job follow-up.

**Rules**
- Reply to new inquiries as fast as you can — ideally within 15 minutes during working hours.
- One ask per message. Always give an easy out.
- Stop the sequence the moment they reply. Stop immediately if they say stop.
- Only text people who contacted you or gave you their number for this purpose.

---

## The sequence at a glance

| When | Channel | Goal |
|------|---------|------|
| Within 15 min of inquiry | Text | Answer + get what you need to quote |
| Same day | Text + email | Send the written estimate with a next step |
| Day 1 | Text | Any questions? |
| Day 3 | Text or email | Offer an option (smaller scope / different date) |
| Day 7 | Text + email | Close the loop politely |
| Day 30 (seasonal work) | Text | Timing check |

---

## Text scripts

**First response (new inquiry)**
> Hi [Name], this is [Your name] with [Business] — thanks for reaching out about [service]! To get you an accurate price, could you send [2–3 photos / the address / the size]? I can usually quote the same day.

**Missed call**
> Hi, this is [Your name] with [Business] — sorry I missed your call, I'm on a job. What can I help with? Texting is the fastest way to reach me.

**Estimate sent (same day)**
> Hi [Name], your estimate for [job] is here: [link]. Total $[__]. I have [day] open — reply YES and I'll book it.

**Day 1**
> Hi [Name], just making sure the estimate came through okay. Any questions about what's included?

**Day 3**
> Hi [Name], checking in on the [job] estimate. If budget's a factor, I can do [smaller scope] for $[__]. Want me to hold [day] for you?

**Day 7 — close the loop**
> Hi [Name], I'll close out your estimate at the end of the week so I can open the slot to others. If you still want it done — now or later — just reply and I'll get you scheduled. Thanks for considering me!

**Day 30 (seasonal services)**
> Hi [Name], still thinking about [job]? [Seasonal reason — e.g., "Spring dates are filling up."] Happy to update your estimate.

## Email scripts

**Same day — Subject: Your [service] estimate from [Business]**
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for the chance to quote your [job]. Your estimate is attached (or here: [link]).
>
> **Total:** $[__]
> **Includes:** [item 1], [item 2], [item 3]
> **Next available:** [day, date]
>
> To book, reply to this email or text me at [phone]. Happy to answer any questions.
>
> [Your name]
> [Business] · [phone]

**Day 3 — Subject: A couple of options for your [job]**
> Hi [Name],
>
> Following up on the estimate I sent [day]. If it helps, here are two options:
>
> - **Full scope** (as quoted): $[__]
> - **[Smaller scope]**: $[__]
>
> I currently have [day] and [day] open. Just reply with what works.
>
> [Your name]

**Day 7 — Subject: Closing out your estimate**
> Hi [Name],
>
> I haven't heard back on the [job] estimate, so I'll close it out at the end of this week. If you'd still like the work done — now or later — reply anytime and I'll update it for you.
>
> Thanks again for considering [Business].
>
> [Your name]

## Handling replies

**"Too expensive."**
> Totally understand. Would it help if I [removed X / split it into two visits / did the most important part first] for $[__]?

**"I went with someone else."**
> Thanks for letting me know — I appreciate it. If you ever need a backup, I'm here.

**"Not right now."**
> No problem at all. Want me to check back in [month]?

---

## Track it

Keep a simple list (a notes app, spreadsheet, or your invoicing app's "open estimates" view) with: name, date quoted, amount, next follow-up date. Review it every morning for two minutes.

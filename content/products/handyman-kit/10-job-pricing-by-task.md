# Job Pricing by Task

A reference for the most common solo-handyman tasks: realistic time ranges, an example price, and what makes each one go sideways. The same task list (with editable hours) is in the **Task Pricing** sheet of `02-handyman-pricing-calculator.xlsx`.

\* **Example price** = typical hours × **$90/hour** + typical materials × 1.2 (20% markup). It's a task price *inside a visit* — a standalone small task still pays your minimum visit charge (default $150). Replace $90 with your own target rate from the **Your Numbers** sheet.

> **License check:** tasks in the plumbing and electrical sections are restricted in many states and cities. Confirm what you're allowed to do before offering them.

## How to use the table

1. **Time yourself.** For the next month, log actual time per task (arrival to cleaned up). Update the "Hours (typical)" column in the sheet.
2. **Price bundles, not tasks.** Put the client's whole list into the Quote Builder; the minimum visit charge only bites when the list is tiny.
3. **Quote ranges for unknowns.** For anything where you can't see inside the wall, quote the typical price plus a written "if we find X, it's $Y more" line.

## Gotchas that blow up the time

| Task type | Gotcha | How to price it |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| TV mounting | Fireplace/brick/tile walls, no studs where the client wants the TV, in-wall power | Masonry: +50–100%. In-wall power is electrical work — licensed trade in many areas |
| Drywall | Texture matching (orange peel, knockdown), plaster walls | Quote texture "best effort"; plaster: hourly |
| Faucets & toilets | Seized supply valves, corroded nuts, rotted subfloor under the toilet | State that valve replacement or subfloor repair is a change order |
| Doors | Out-of-square frames, house settling, non-standard sizes | Inspect before quoting slab-only replacements |
| Furniture assembly | Missing parts, stripped cam locks, 200-piece wardrobes | Quote by item size; missing parts = return trip billed |
| Deck/fence | Rot extends beyond the visible board | Quote per board with "additional boards at $X each" |
| Old homes (pre-1978) | Lead paint when sanding or cutting | Follow EPA RRP rules — certification is required for paid work that disturbs lead paint in pre-1978 homes |

## Price floors to protect

- Never quote a standalone visit below your **minimum visit charge**.
- Never quote below **your target hourly × typical hours** — the Quote Builder's "Status" cell flags it.
- Add materials markup even on cheap parts; the pickup time is real.

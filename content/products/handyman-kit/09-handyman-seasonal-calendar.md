# Handyman Seasonal Upsell & Marketing Calendar

| Month | What homeowners are thinking about | Offer | Marketing action |
|-------|-----------------------------------|-------|------------------|
| **Jan** | Post-holiday fixes, new-year projects | TV/shelf mounting for new gifts; closet systems; "fix list" half-day | Text past clients: "Send me your 2026 fix list" |
| **Feb** | Indoor projects, tax refunds coming | Drywall repair + paint touch-up; bathroom caulk & grout refresh | Post before/after of a caulk refresh |
| **Mar** | Spring prep | Screen repair, door weatherstripping check, deck board inspection | Offer a "spring home check" (1 hour: walk-through + small fixes + written list) |
| **Apr** | Outdoor projects, selling homes | Pre-listing repair packages; fence and deck repairs | Introduce yourself to 3 local real estate agents with a pre-listing punch-list price sheet |
| **May** | Outdoor living | Deck repair/seal, patio furniture assembly, outdoor lighting (where permitted) | Neighborhood groups: offer to bundle outdoor tasks |
| **Jun** | Move season | Move-in packages: mount TVs, hang art, assemble furniture, change locks/hardware | Partner with a local moving company or property manager |
| **Jul** | Vacations, heat | Indoor work; ceiling fan swaps (where permitted); blinds | Reactivate past clients who haven't booked in 6+ months |
| **Aug** | Back-to-school | Desk/furniture assembly, dorm-room kits, kids' room shelving, baby-proofing | Local parent groups |
| **Sep** | Fall prep | Gutter cleaning (1-story), weatherstripping, door adjustments, caulk windows | "Fall home check" offer |
| **Oct** | Pre-holiday hosting | Guest room fixes, hanging décor hooks, grab bars for visiting parents | Promote "get ready for guests" half-day |
| **Nov** | Holidays | Holiday light hanging (if insured for ladder work), furniture assembly | Sell gift certificates ("an afternoon of Dad's fix list") |
| **Dec** | Busy early, slow late | Gift assembly (Dec 26–31), TV mounting | Plan January price update; send thank-you notes to top clients |

## Standing offers that work year-round
- **Half-day / full-day blocks** for repeat clients with a list
- **Landlord/property manager turnover package**: patch, paint touch-up, hardware, caulk, smoke detector checks
- **Aging-in-place basics**: grab bars (into studs/blocking), lever handles, better lighting, handrails — high value and often referral-driven

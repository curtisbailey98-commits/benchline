# Benchline Handyman Trade Kit — Start Here

This kit applies the Benchline system to solo handyman work: price each task honestly, control scope on site, and put the important things in writing.

## Set it up in one afternoon (about 3 hours)

| Step | Time | File | What to do |
|------|------|------|------------|
| 1 | 20 min | `02-handyman-pricing-calculator.xlsx` → **Your Numbers** | Enter pay goal, costs, billable hours → note your target hourly rate |
| 2 | 45 min | `02-...xlsx` → **Task Pricing** | Adjust hours for tasks you do often; delete tasks you don't offer; set minimum visit charge and markup |
| 3 | 15 min | `01-handyman-pricing-guide.md` + `10-job-pricing-by-task.md` | Decide your pricing policy (minimum, hourly after, materials, trip charge) |
| 4 | 20 min | **Check your license limits** | Look up your state/county handyman rules (job-value caps, electrical/plumbing restrictions). Write your "we don't do" list. |
| 5 | 20 min | `05-handyman-service-agreement.md` | Fill in policies; have a local attorney review |
| 6 | 15 min | `11-change-order-form.md` | Print a pad or save as a fillable PDF on your phone |
| 7 | 20 min | `07-...` + `08-...` | Build your intake form; save text scripts as shortcuts |
| 8 | 15 min | `06-...` + `09-...` | Print checklists; put the next 3 months of seasonal offers on your calendar |

## How the files fit together

Lead → intake questions (07) → quote with Quote Builder (02) → estimate (04) → agreement (05) → checklists (06) → change orders when scope grows (11) → follow-up and review texts (08) → seasonal offers (09).

## Important: licensing

Many states and cities limit what an unlicensed handyman can do — often a dollar cap per job and restrictions on electrical, plumbing, gas, HVAC, and structural work. **Look up your rules before you quote.** Tasks in this kit marked "check license limits" may require a licensed trade where you work. This kit is not legal advice.

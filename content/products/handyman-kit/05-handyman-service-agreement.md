# Handyman Service Agreement

> **Not legal advice.** This is a plain-language template for solo handyman businesses. Home improvement contracts are regulated differently in each state (some require specific notices, cancellation rights, license numbers, or deposit limits). **Have a licensed attorney in your state review and adapt it before use.**

---

**Business:** [Business name] · [license/registration # if required] · [address] · [phone] · [email]
**Client:** [Name] · [job address] · [phone] · [email]
**Estimate #:** [___] (attached and part of this agreement)

## 1. Scope

We'll perform the work in the attached estimate. Anything not listed is not included. Added or changed work requires a signed or texted/emailed approval of a **change order** stating the added price and time.

## 2. Licensing limits

We perform work permitted for our license/registration type in [state/county]. We don't perform work that legally requires a licensed electrician, plumber, HVAC, gas, or general contractor, and we won't pull permits for such work. If your project needs a licensed trade, we'll tell you.

## 3. Price and payment

- Total price: $[___] per the estimate. Hourly work: $[___]/hour in [30]-minute increments with a [1]-hour minimum.
- Deposit: $[___] due to schedule [only if permitted and needed; note any state limit]. Balance due on completion.
- Materials we purchase are billed at receipt cost plus [__]%.
- Late payments: invoices unpaid after [7/14] days incur [a late fee of $__ / __% per month, as permitted by law].

## 4. Schedule and access

- Start date / arrival window: [___]. Weather, material delays, or hidden conditions may affect timing; we'll keep you updated.
- You'll provide safe access to the work area, working electricity and water, and a place to park.
- Please clear the work area of valuables and fragile items.

## 5. Hidden conditions

If we uncover conditions we couldn't reasonably see (rot, leaks, damaged wiring, pests, mold, asbestos, lead paint), we'll stop work in that area and discuss options. Additional work proceeds only by change order. We're not responsible for pre-existing conditions.

## 6. Client-supplied materials

If you supply materials or fixtures, you're responsible for their quality, correct size, and compatibility. Extra trips caused by wrong or missing parts are billed at $[___]. We don't warranty client-supplied materials.

## 7. Workmanship warranty

We warranty our labor for **[90 days]** from completion against defects in workmanship. If a problem covered by this warranty appears, contact us and we'll fix it at no labor charge. This warranty doesn't cover: client-supplied parts, manufacturer defects, misuse, normal wear, damage by others, or pre-existing conditions. [Check whether your state implies other warranties.]

## 8. Damage and insurance

We carry general liability insurance [carrier; certificate on request]. Report any damage you believe we caused within [48] hours. We're not liable for damage resulting from pre-existing conditions, concealed defects, or items you asked us to handle against our advice.

## 9. Cancellations

- You may cancel [within 3 business days of signing, if your state gives a cooling-off right — confirm and add required language].
- Cancellations with less than [24/48] hours' notice before scheduled work may be charged a $[___] fee, plus any non-returnable materials purchased for your job.

## 10. Cleanup

We'll leave the work area broom-clean and remove our debris [haul-away of old fixtures/materials: included / $__].

## 11. Photos

We may photograph the work before, during, and after for our records. We'll only share photos publicly with your written permission.

## 12. Disputes

We'll try to resolve any disagreement directly first. [Optional: mediation clause.] [State] law governs this agreement.

## 13. Entire agreement

This agreement, the estimate, and any signed change orders are the complete agreement.

# Handyman Text Scripts

---

## Before the job

**Estimate sent**
> Hi [Name], your estimate for [job] is here: [link]. Total: $[__]. Reply "Approved" and I'll get you on the schedule — my next opening is [day].

**Estimate follow-up (2 days, no reply)**
> Hi [Name], just checking you got the estimate for [job]. Happy to adjust scope if it helps with budget. Want me to hold [day] for you?

**Parts check (day before)**
> Hi [Name], confirming tomorrow between [window]. Do you have the [faucet / mount / fixture] on site? If you can, send a photo of the box so I can bring the right fittings.

**On my way**
> On my way — about [__] minutes out.

## During the job

**Hidden damage found**
> Hi [Name], when I opened up [area] I found [rot / a slow leak / damaged wiring]. Photos attached. Fixing it properly adds about [__] hours and $[__]. I've written it up as a change order — I'll wait for your OK before I go further.

**Running long**
> Heads-up: [task] is taking longer than planned because [reason]. I expect to finish around [time]. Price stays as quoted unless we add work.

## After the job

**Day-of**
> Thanks for having me, [Name]. Everything's done: [list]. My workmanship is covered for [90 days] — if anything's not right, text me.

**3-day check-in**
> Hi [Name], how's the [TV / faucet / door] holding up? Any drips or wobbles, let me know.

**Review request (after a positive reply)**
> Glad to hear it! If you have a minute, a Google review helps neighbors find a handyman who shows up: [link]. Thanks either way.

**Next-project prompt (2–3 weeks later)**
> Hi [Name], if there's anything else on the honey-do list, I'm booking half-day visits in [month]. Send me the list and I'll quote it as one visit.

**Referral ask (for happy repeat clients)**
> Hi [Name], if you know anyone who needs a reliable handyman, I'd appreciate the referral — I always save a spot for clients' friends.

## Money

**Invoice reminder (day 7)**
> Hi [Name], friendly reminder that the invoice for [job] ($[__]) is still open: [link]. Thanks!

**Invoice reminder (day 14)**
> Hi [Name], following up on the [job] invoice from [date], $[__]. Can you let me know when to expect payment? A late fee applies after [date] per our agreement.

## Saying no

**Outside license limits**
> Thanks for thinking of me, [Name]. That work needs a licensed [electrician / plumber] in our state, so I can't take it on. I'm happy to handle [the parts I can do], and I can suggest someone for the rest.

**Too small / too far**
> I appreciate it! That one's under my minimum visit, so it'd cost you $[__]. If you have a couple more items, I can bundle them into one visit and make it worth it.

*Benchline Handyman Trade Kit.*

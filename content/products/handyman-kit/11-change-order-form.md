## How to introduce a change order without awkwardness

**When the client adds work on site:**
> "Happy to do that. Since it's not on today's estimate, I'll write it up as a quick change order so the price is clear — it's $[__]. Want me to add it?"

**When you find hidden damage:**
> "I want to show you something before I go further." *(Show photos.)* "Fixing this properly adds about [__] hours and $[__]. I can do it today with your OK, or leave it and finish the original scope. Your call."

**When the client asks for a "small favor":**
> "That's a 10-minute thing — I'll just add it to the change order at my half-hour minimum so everything's on paper."

**Rules**
1. Write it before you do it. Text a photo of the form if you're not using an app.
2. One change order per change — don't bundle surprises.
3. Always show the **new job total**, not just the added amount.
4. Keep copies with the job file for disputes and taxes.

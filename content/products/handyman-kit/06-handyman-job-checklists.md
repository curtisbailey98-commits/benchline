# Handyman Job Checklists

---

## A. Truck loadout — core kit (always on board)

**Hand tools:** tape measure (25'), 4' and torpedo levels, stud finder, utility knife + blades, multi-bit screwdriver, nut drivers, pliers set (lineman, needle-nose, channel-lock), adjustable wrenches (2), basin wrench, hammer, flat bar, putty knives (1.5", 6"), caulk gun, hacksaw, speed square, pencil + marker.

**Power tools (charged batteries ×2):** drill/driver, impact driver, oscillating multi-tool, circular saw, jigsaw, shop vac.

**Consumables:** assorted screws (drywall, wood, cabinet), wall anchors (plastic, toggle, self-drilling), wire nuts (only if you're permitted to do electrical), plumber's tape, silicone + paintable caulk, wood glue, spackle, setting-type joint compound, sandpaper/sanding sponges, painter's tape, shims, zip ties.

**Protection & safety:** drop cloths, floor runners, shoe covers, safety glasses, hearing protection, dust masks/respirator, gloves, non-contact voltage tester, first-aid kit, fire extinguisher.

**Admin:** tablet/phone with estimate app, change-order pad (file 11), card reader, business cards.

## B. Before you leave for the job

- [ ] Re-read the estimate and client notes — any special parts needed?
- [ ] Confirm client-supplied parts are on site (text the client the day before)
- [ ] Load job-specific materials; pull receipts into the job folder
- [ ] Ladder height right for this job?

## C. Arrival

- [ ] Park so you don't block the client; ask where to stage
- [ ] Shoe covers or indoor shoes; lay floor runners to the work area
- [ ] Walk the scope with the client **against the estimate** — confirm each line
- [ ] **Photo the work area before starting**, especially existing damage
- [ ] Ask: "Anything else on your list today?" → price it before you start (change order)
- [ ] Locate shutoffs if doing plumbing (water) — and confirm breaker labeling before any electrical work you're permitted to do

## D. During the job

- [ ] Protect surfaces (drop cloth under every drilling point)
- [ ] Test before you close up: water on and check for drips (10 min), fixture works, door latches
- [ ] If hidden damage appears: stop, photo, explain, change order
- [ ] Keep the client informed if you'll run past the estimated time

## E. Closeout

- [ ] Clean up: vacuum dust, remove packaging, wipe fingerprints
- [ ] Demonstrate to the client (TV level, faucet no drips, door closes)
- [ ] **After photos**
- [ ] Walk the list — every line done, change orders signed
- [ ] Collect payment or send invoice before leaving
- [ ] Mention warranty terms and how to reach you
- [ ] Ask about the next project ("What's next on the list?")

## F. After the job

- [ ] Day-of follow-up text (file 08)
- [ ] Log actual hours per task → update the Task Pricing sheet quarterly
- [ ] Restock consumables; charge batteries
- [ ] File receipts to the job for materials markup and taxes

*Benchline Handyman Trade Kit.*

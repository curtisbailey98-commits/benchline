# Residential Cleaning Service Agreement

> **Not legal advice.** This is a plain-language starting template for solo cleaning businesses. Laws on contracts, deposits, cancellation, and consumer protection vary by state and city. **Have a licensed attorney in your state review and adapt it before you use it.**

---

**Business:** [Business name] ("we", "us") · [address] · [phone] · [email]
**Client:** [Name] ("you") · [service address] · [phone] · [email]
**Effective date:** [date]

## 1. Services

We will provide the cleaning services described in the accepted estimate #[___] ("Services"). Changes to scope are agreed in writing (text or email is fine) and may change the price.

## 2. Schedule

- Frequency: [weekly / every 2 weeks / every 4 weeks / one-time]
- Day and arrival window: [e.g., Tuesdays, 9:00–11:00 AM]. Arrival windows allow for traffic and prior jobs.
- Holidays: if a visit falls on [list holidays], we'll offer the nearest available day.

## 3. Price and payment

- Price per visit: $[___]. First-visit deep clean: $[___].
- Payment is due [on completion / charged to the card on file after each visit]. Invoices unpaid after [7] days incur a late fee of $[__] [confirm your state's limits on late fees].
- We may adjust recurring prices with **30 days' written notice**. You may cancel before the new price takes effect.

## 4. Access and lockouts

- You'll provide access by [key / lockbox / code / someone home]. Keys are labeled with a code, never your address.
- If we can't get in at the scheduled time (no one home, wrong code, locked gate), we'll call/text and wait [15] minutes. After that, a **lockout fee of $[__] or [50]% of the visit price** applies.

## 5. Cancellations and rescheduling

- Please give at least **[24/48] hours' notice** to cancel or reschedule.
- Late cancellations are charged [50]% of the visit price. Same-day cancellations and lockouts are charged [the lockout fee].
- We'll give you as much notice as possible if we need to reschedule (illness, severe weather, vehicle breakdown), and you won't be charged.

## 6. Pets

- Please tell us about all pets. Aggressive or anxious pets should be secured during the visit.
- We won't clean pet waste beyond normal litter-box areas, and we may end a visit (and charge for it) if we can't work safely.

## 7. Home condition, safety, and what we won't do

- We don't move furniture over [40] lbs, climb ladders higher than [2] steps, clean exterior windows above the first floor, or handle biohazards (bodily fluids, needles, mold remediation, pest infestations).
- We may decline or stop any task we consider unsafe.
- If the home's condition is significantly different from what was described when we quoted, we'll discuss scope and price before continuing.

## 8. Breakage and damage

- We're careful, and we carry general liability insurance [insurer / policy # on request].
- Please tell us about fragile, valuable, or sentimental items; we recommend putting away irreplaceable items.
- Report any damage within **[24/48] hours** of the visit. We'll repair, replace, or reimburse at our discretion up to [$___ / the item's actual cash value].
- We're not responsible for damage from normal wear, improperly installed fixtures, pre-existing damage, or items that fail from ordinary cleaning (e.g., already-loose fixtures).

## 9. Satisfaction

If something was missed, tell us within **24 hours** with a photo if possible. We'll return to re-clean the missed area at no charge within [2] business days. Re-cleans are our remedy for missed areas; we don't issue refunds for completed visits unless required by law.

## 10. Supplies and products

We bring our own supplies and equipment [list exceptions: e.g., we use your vacuum if requested]. If you want specific products used, please provide them. Tell us about any surfaces that need special care (natural stone, unsealed wood, antiques).

## 11. Privacy

We don't photograph the inside of your home except to document damage, issues, or before/after results for your records, and we don't post photos of your home without written permission.

## 12. Non-solicitation of staff (if you ever add helpers)

[Optional — remove if solo.] During this agreement and for [6] months after, you agree not to hire our team members directly. [Check whether this is enforceable in your state.]

## 13. Term and ending the agreement

Either party may end recurring service with **[14]** days' written notice. Any completed visits remain payable.

## 14. General

This agreement and the accepted estimate are the whole agreement. If one part is unenforceable, the rest still applies. [State] law governs.

## What's included in every visit

**Kitchen:** counters and backsplash wiped, exterior of appliances and cabinets spot-cleaned, sink scrubbed and polished, stovetop degreased, microwave inside and out, floors vacuumed and mopped, trash emptied.

**Bathrooms:** toilet inside and out (including base), tub / shower scrubbed, sink and counters, mirrors, fixtures polished, floors mopped, trash emptied.

**Bedrooms & living areas:** dust reachable surfaces and décor, mirrors and glass tables, make beds (linens changed if left out — add-on), vacuum carpets and rugs, vacuum/mop hard floors, trash emptied.

**Deep clean adds:** baseboards hand-wiped, door frames and switch plates, cabinet fronts wiped, light fixtures dusted (reachable), vents dusted, behind/under movable furniture, detailed grout scrub.

# Cleaning Seasonal Upsell & Marketing Calendar

One focus per month. Plan the promo 4–6 weeks before the month it's for — people book cleaning ahead of events.

| Month | Demand pattern | Upsell / offer | Marketing action |
|-------|---------------|----------------|------------------|
| **Jan** | Post-holiday reset, resolutions | "New Year reset" deep clean; convert holiday one-timers to recurring | Text every December one-time client; offer the recurring price |
| **Feb** | Slower month | Inside fridge + oven bundle; closet/pantry organization add-on | Ask recurring clients for referrals (offer a credit on their next visit) |
| **Mar** | Spring cleaning begins | Spring deep clean: baseboards, blinds, interior windows, light fixtures | Post a spring checklist on social / neighborhood groups; book April out |
| **Apr** | Peak spring demand | Interior windows + tracks, wall spot-cleaning | Raise one-time prices modestly; waitlist overflow into recurring |
| **May** | Move season starts, graduations | Move-out/move-in cleans; pre-party cleans | Introduce yourself to 3 local property managers and real estate agents |
| **Jun** | Moves peak, summer schedules | Move-out cleans; vacation "come home to a clean house" | Offer "while you're away" cleans to recurring clients |
| **Jul** | Vacations, some cancellations | Fill gaps with one-time deep cleans | Reactivate lapsed clients (see win-back texts) |
| **Aug** | Back-to-school | Kid-room + mudroom reset, pantry clean-out | Text families on your list mid-month |
| **Sep** | Routines restart | Convert summer one-timers to recurring | Ask for Google reviews from your happiest recurring clients |
| **Oct** | Pre-holiday planning | Book holiday deep cleans now; oven + fridge before Thanksgiving | Announce holiday booking window; hold spots for recurring clients first |
| **Nov** | Holiday hosting | Pre-guest cleans, post-party cleans, gift certificates | Sell gift certificates (clean for a parent, new baby, new homeowner) |
| **Dec** | Busy first half, slow last week | Post-holiday reset bookings for early January | Send thank-you notes; plan January price changes (30 days' notice) |

## Year-round habits
- Keep a **waitlist** during busy months — it becomes next month's recurring clients.
- Review your recurring price every January; give 30 days' notice.
- Track which months you had gaps last year and start that month's promo earlier.

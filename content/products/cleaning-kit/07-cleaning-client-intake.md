# Cleaning Client Intake Questions

Use these on the first call or as your online booking form. Each question exists because the answer changes the price, the time, or the risk.

---

## Contact
1. Name, mobile number, email
2. Service address (and the neighborhood / cross streets for routing)
3. Best way to reach you: text / call / email
4. How did you hear about us? (Tracks which marketing works)

## The home
5. Bedrooms / full baths / half baths
6. Approximate square footage and number of floors
7. Flooring types: carpet, hardwood, tile, luxury vinyl, natural stone?
8. Any surfaces that need special care (marble, unsealed wood, antique furniture)?
9. How many people live there? Any kids under 5?
10. Pets: how many, what kind, do they shed, will they be home and loose?

## Condition (ask kindly — no judgment)
11. When was the home last professionally cleaned?
12. On a scale of 1–5, how would you describe it right now? (1 = just cleaned, 5 = needs a full reset)
13. Anything specific that bothers you most? (Soap scum, pet hair, kitchen grease, baseboards)
14. Any clutter we'd need to work around? Will surfaces be cleared before we arrive?
15. Can you text 2–3 photos of the kitchen and main bathroom? (Helps us quote accurately)

## Service
16. Type: one-time, deep clean, move-out/move-in, or recurring?
17. If recurring: weekly, every 2 weeks, or every 4 weeks? Preferred day and time?
18. Add-ons you're interested in: inside oven, inside fridge, interior windows, blinds, cabinets, laundry, linens?
19. Any rooms that are off-limits?
20. Do you want us to use any specific products (allergies, fragrance-free, eco-only)?

## Access
21. Will someone be home? If not: key, lockbox, door code, garage code?
22. Alarm system? (We'll need the code and instructions)
23. Parking: driveway, street, permit needed?

## Move-out specific
24. Is the home empty? When are keys due back?
25. Is this for a landlord / property manager checklist? (Ask them to send it)
26. Are utilities (water, power) still on? (We can't clean without them)

## Close the call

> "Thanks — based on this I'll send a written estimate by [time today]. For a first clean, I usually quote a deep clean, and then your recurring price after that. Sound good?"

**Red flags that call for an in-person walkthrough or hourly pricing:** condition 5, heavy clutter, active pest problems, post-construction dust, "just do what you can in 2 hours," or refusal to share photos.

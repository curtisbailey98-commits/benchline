# Cleaning Job Checklists

Print, laminate, keep in the caddy. Tick with a dry-erase marker.

---

## A. Caddy + vehicle loadout (check the night before)

**Chemicals (labeled, capped, secondary containers labeled too)**
- [ ] All-purpose cleaner
- [ ] Glass cleaner
- [ ] Bathroom / soap-scum cleaner
- [ ] Degreaser (kitchen)
- [ ] Disinfectant (follow label dwell time)
- [ ] Stone-safe / pH-neutral cleaner (granite, marble)
- [ ] Wood cleaner for sealed floors
- [ ] Oven cleaner (only if oven add-on booked)

> Never mix bleach with ammonia or acids. Keep the Safety Data Sheets (SDS) for your products in the car or on your phone.

**Tools**
- [ ] Microfiber cloths color-coded: **red** = toilets, **yellow** = bathroom surfaces, **blue** = glass, **green** = kitchen, **white** = dusting
- [ ] Scrub brushes, grout brush, toilet brush (bagged)
- [ ] Pumice stone (porcelain only), non-scratch scrub pads
- [ ] Extendable duster, crevice tools
- [ ] Vacuum (HEPA) + attachments, spare bags/filter
- [ ] Mop + clean pads (one per floor type), bucket
- [ ] Step stool (2-step max)
- [ ] Trash bags, gloves, shoe covers, knee pad
- [ ] Laminated room-by-room checklist (file 10)

**Admin**
- [ ] Today's addresses, access codes, and pet notes
- [ ] Phone charged; card reader / invoice app working
- [ ] Leave-behind cards with review QR

## B. Arrival (first 5 minutes)

- [ ] Park legally; don't block driveways
- [ ] Shoe covers on at the door
- [ ] Greet client (if home); confirm any special requests for today
- [ ] Quick walk-through: note anything broken, stained, or different from last time — **photo pre-existing damage**
- [ ] Pets secured / accounted for
- [ ] Set up caddy in a central spot; plan the route (top to bottom, back to front)

## C. Order of work

1. Start what needs dwell time: spray tubs/showers, toilets, oven (if booked).
2. Dust high → low in every room.
3. Kitchen: counters, appliances, sink last.
4. Bathrooms: rinse dwell-time surfaces, mirrors, counters, toilet last.
5. Bedrooms and living areas.
6. Floors last, working toward the exit: vacuum everything, then mop.

## D. Closeout (last 10 minutes)

- [ ] Walk every room with the checklist — lights off, fans back as found
- [ ] Trash out (to the client's bin, not left by the door)
- [ ] Doors locked, alarm set, key back in lockbox
- [ ] "After" photos of the kitchen and main bathroom (for your records)
- [ ] Leave a card or note: what you did, anything you noticed ("the bathroom faucet is dripping")
- [ ] Take payment / confirm card charge

## E. After the visit

- [ ] Send the day-of text (file 08)
- [ ] Log actual time vs. quoted time — update the Quote Builder if you're consistently over
- [ ] Wash microfiber (no fabric softener), restock chemicals
- [ ] Note any add-on opportunities for next visit (oven looked rough, blinds dusty)

# Benchline Cleaning Trade Kit — Start Here

This kit applies the Benchline system to residential cleaning. Every file is plain Markdown, CSV, or XLSX — paste into Notion or Google Docs, open the spreadsheet in Excel / Google Sheets / LibreOffice / Numbers, or print.

## Set it up in one afternoon (about 3 hours)

| Step | Time | File | What to do |
|------|------|------|------------|
| 1 | 30 min | `02-cleaning-pricing-calculator.xlsx` → **Your Numbers** | Enter your pay goal, costs, and billable hours. Note your **target hourly rate**. |
| 2 | 30 min | `02-...xlsx` → **Quote Builder** | Time your own cleans for a week, then replace the minute benchmarks with yours. |
| 3 | 20 min | `03-cleaning-rate-card.csv` + `01-cleaning-pricing-guide.md` | Set your minimums and add-on prices. Delete lines you won't offer. |
| 4 | 20 min | `05-cleaning-service-agreement.md` | Fill in your policies (lockouts, pets, cancellations). Get it reviewed locally. |
| 5 | 20 min | `07-cleaning-client-intake.md` | Turn the questions into a booking form (Google Forms, Jotform, your scheduling app). |
| 6 | 20 min | `08-cleaning-text-scripts.md` | Save the texts as phone shortcuts or canned replies. |
| 7 | 20 min | `06-...` and `10-room-by-room-checklist.md` | Print, laminate, keep in the caddy. |
| 8 | 20 min | `09-cleaning-seasonal-calendar.md` | Put the next 3 months of promos on your calendar. |

## How the files fit together

1. **Lead comes in** → ask the intake questions (07).
2. **Quote** → run the Quote Builder or Move-Out sheet (02, 11), send the estimate (04).
3. **Book** → client accepts the service agreement (05).
4. **Clean** → checklists (06, 10).
5. **After** → follow-up, rebooking, and review texts (08).
6. **Every month** → check the seasonal calendar (09) for the next upsell.

## Important notes

- Prices in this kit are **starting ranges**, not market data for your city. Call three local competitors as a "customer" or check their websites before you finalize.
- The service agreement is a template, **not legal advice**. Laws vary by state and city — have a local attorney review it.
- Insurance: most residential clients will ask whether you're insured and bonded. Get general liability coverage before you take keys.

# Cleaning Text Scripts

Short, human, one ask per message. Replace [brackets]. Only text clients who've given you their number for service updates, and stop immediately if they ask.

---

## Booking

**Confirmation (when booked)**
> Hi [Name], you're booked for a [deep / standard] clean on [day, date], arriving between [window]. Total: $[__]. Reply here with any access notes or questions. — [Your name], [Business]

**Reminder (day before)**
> Hi [Name], reminder: I'll be there tomorrow between [window]. If you can, please clear counters and pick up clothes/toys so I can clean every surface. See you then!

**On my way**
> On my way — I'll be there in about [__] minutes.

**Running late (text before you're late, not after)**
> Hi [Name], I'm running about [__] minutes behind from my last home. Still okay to come by [new time]? Sorry for the shift.

## After the first clean

**Day-of (within 2 hours)**
> Hi [Name], all done! I focused on [the kitchen grease / bathroom soap scum / baseboards]. If anything looks missed, text me a photo within 24 hours and I'll come back and fix it.

**24-hour check-in**
> Morning [Name] — how's the house looking today? Anything you'd like me to focus on next time?

**Convert a one-time client to recurring (day 2–3)**
> Hi [Name], glad the clean helped. Most clients keep it this way with a visit every 2 weeks — that's $[__] per visit, and your first-clean reset is already done. I have [day] mornings open. Want me to hold one for you?

**If they say "maybe later"**
> No problem at all. I'll check back in a few weeks. Thanks again for having me!

## Review request

**Only after a positive reply.**
> So glad to hear it! If you have a minute, a Google review really helps other families find a cleaner they can trust: [review link]. Thanks either way, [Name].

**Gentle reminder (once, 3 days later, only if they said yes)**
> Hi [Name], here's that review link again in case it got buried: [link]. No worries if you're busy!

## Recurring client touches

**Skipped visit follow-up**
> Hi [Name], we missed your clean this week — want me to fit one in [day] or just see you at your next regular visit on [date]?

**Add-on suggestion (after you notice something)**
> Hi [Name], I noticed the oven could use a good clean. I can do the inside next visit for $[__] — want me to add it?

**Holiday / guests (late Oct–Nov)**
> Hi [Name], hosting for the holidays? I'm booking extra deep cleans and pre-guest cleans for [month]. Want me to hold a spot before [date]?

**Price increase (30 days' notice)**
> Hi [Name], a heads-up: starting [date], my rate for your every-2-weeks clean will be $[new] (from $[old]). Supply and fuel costs have gone up and I want to keep giving you the same careful clean. Thank you for being a client — it means a lot.

## Problems

**Missed spot complaint**
> Thank you for telling me, and I'm sorry that was missed. I can come back [day/time] to take care of it at no charge. Does that work?

**Lockout**
> Hi [Name], I'm at your home for your [time] clean but can't get in — [the code isn't working / no one answered]. I'll wait until [time + 15 min]. If I can't get in by then, the lockout fee in our agreement applies and we'll reschedule.

**Stop**
> Understood — I won't text again. Thanks for the opportunity.

*Benchline Cleaning Trade Kit.*

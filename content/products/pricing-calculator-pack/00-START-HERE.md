# Benchline Pricing Calculator Pack — Start Here

Five calculators in one spreadsheet (`01-benchline-pricing-calculators.xlsx`) plus the words to use when you present, defend, and raise your prices. Works for any trade.

| Calculator (sheet) | Use it when | Time |
|--------------------|------------|------|
| **1 · Hourly Target** | Once now, then every January | 20 min |
| **2 · Job Profit Check** | Before sending any quote you're unsure about | 2 min |
| **3 · Drive Time Cost** | Setting a service radius and trip fees | 10 min |
| **4 · Break-Even** | Planning how many jobs you need per week | 5 min |
| **5 · Price Increase** | Before a price increase — how many clients can you afford to lose? | 5 min |

**Yellow cells are inputs. Green cells are results.** Sheets 2–4 pull your target rate and costs from sheet 1, so fill that in first.

Opens in Excel, Google Sheets (File → Import), LibreOffice, and Numbers.

## Reading order

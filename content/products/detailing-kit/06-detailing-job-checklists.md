# Mobile Detailing Job Checklists

---

## A. Van / trailer loadout (night before)

**Water & power**
- [ ] Water tank full (and a secured cap); pump primed
- [ ] Generator fueled and tested **or** extension cords + inverter charged
- [ ] Pressure washer (electric or gas), hose reel, foam cannon
- [ ] Water reclamation mat (where runoff rules require)

**Wash**
- [ ] Two buckets with grit guards, wash mitts (separate one for lower panels)
- [ ] Wheel bucket, wheel brushes, tire brush, wheel woolies
- [ ] pH-neutral shampoo, wheel cleaner, tire cleaner, iron remover, bug & tar remover
- [ ] Clay bar/mitt + lubricant
- [ ] Drying towels (clean, tag-free), air blower

**Interior**
- [ ] Vacuum + crevice/brush tools, compressed-air blow gun
- [ ] Hot-water extractor (if booked), upholstery and carpet cleaner, enzyme cleaner
- [ ] All-purpose cleaner (diluted for interior), interior detailer, leather cleaner + conditioner
- [ ] Detailing brushes (soft for vents/screens), steam cleaner (optional)
- [ ] Pet hair tools (rubber brush, pumice-style stone for carpets only)
- [ ] Glass cleaner (ammonia-free — safe on tint), glass towels

**Protection & paint**
- [ ] Spray sealant / wax, tire dressing, trim restorer
- [ ] Polisher, pads (cutting/polishing/finishing), compound & polish, panel wipe, paint depth gauge (paint enhancement jobs)
- [ ] Masking tape for trim when polishing

**Admin & safety**
- [ ] Vehicle condition inspection forms (file 11) or app
- [ ] Gloves, eye protection, respirator for ozone/chemicals, knee pads
- [ ] Card reader, business cards, bags for loose personal items

## B. Arrival

- [ ] Confirm the vehicle, package, and add-ons with the client
- [ ] Ask about warning lights, fragile trim, aftermarket parts, alarm/keyless quirks
- [ ] **Walk-around inspection** — fill in the condition form, take photos of all 4 corners + interior + any damage, get the client's signature
- [ ] Confirm the condition level matches the quote; re-quote if not **before** starting
- [ ] Park your rig safely; set up mat, cones if in a lot
- [ ] Bag loose personal items; leave them in the trunk or with the client

## C. Service order (full detail)

1. **Interior first** (keeps dust off clean paint): remove mats → blow out → vacuum top to bottom → clean plastics, vents, screens → seats (extract/condition) → carpets → door jambs → glass last.
2. **Wheels & tires** first on the exterior.
3. **Pre-rinse / foam**, then two-bucket hand wash top to bottom.
4. **Decontaminate** (iron remover, clay) if in the package.
5. **Dry** with blower + towels (door jambs, mirrors, trim).
6. **Protect:** sealant/wax, trim, tire dressing.
7. **Glass** inside and out.
8. **Mats** back in, dry.

## D. Closeout

- [ ] Final walk-around in good light (use a flashlight for swirls/streaks)
- [ ] **After photos** from the same angles
- [ ] Walk the client around the car; point out anything that improved but isn't perfect (as disclosed)
- [ ] Return keys and personal items
- [ ] Take payment; offer a maintenance plan
- [ ] Pack up; leave the parking spot cleaner than you found it

## E. After

- [ ] Day-of text + review request after a positive reply (file 08)
- [ ] Log actual hours vs. estimate → update the Package Menu hours
- [ ] Wash microfiber (no softener), clean pads, restock chemicals
- [ ] Refill water tank

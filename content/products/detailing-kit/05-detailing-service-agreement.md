# Mobile Detailing Service Agreement

> **Not legal advice.** A plain-language template for solo mobile detailers. Rules on contracts, liability limits, and consumer protection differ by state. **Have a licensed attorney in your state review and adapt it before use.** Use with `11-vehicle-condition-inspection.md`.

**Client:** [Name] · [phone] · [email]

## 1. Services
We'll perform the package and add-ons in the accepted estimate/booking. Changes on the day (upgrades or add-ons) are confirmed before we begin them.

## 2. Vehicle condition inspection
Before starting, we'll walk around the vehicle and record existing damage and condition (scratches, chips, dents, curb rash, stains, tears, odors, warning lights) on the **Vehicle Condition Inspection** form with photos. You'll be asked to review and sign it. We're not responsible for pre-existing conditions recorded there.

## 3. Personal items
Please remove valuables and personal items. We'll bag loose items we find and leave them in the vehicle, but we're not responsible for items left in the vehicle, including cash, electronics, or documents.

## 4. Results
- We'll perform services with reasonable care using professional products and methods.
- Stains, odors, pet hair, and paint defects are improved, not always eliminated. Deep scratches, rock chips, clear-coat failure, and permanent stains can't be corrected by detailing.
- Some interior materials (old leather, headliners, aftermarket parts, loose trim) can be fragile; we'll tell you about any concerns before cleaning them.

## 5. Price, deposits, and payment
- Price per the estimate: $[___]. If the vehicle's condition is significantly worse than described or shown in photos, we'll quote the difference and get your approval before starting (or you may cancel with no charge other than [travel fee $__]).
- Deposit: $[___] [if applicable], applied to the final price.
- Payment due on completion.

## 6. Cancellations and no-shows
- Please give at least [24] hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or no-shows (including vehicle not available at the scheduled time and place) may be charged $[___] [or the deposit].
- We may reschedule for weather, and there's no charge to you.

## 7. Site and water
You confirm you have permission to have the vehicle washed at the service location. We follow applicable runoff rules [describe your practice: water reclamation mat, rinseless methods, etc.].

## 8. Damage and insurance
- We carry [general liability and garage keepers / care-custody-control] insurance [carrier; proof on request].
- Report any concern about damage within [24] hours of service, with photos. We'll inspect, compare with the condition inspection, and, if caused by our negligence, repair or reimburse reasonable costs.
- We're not responsible for failures of pre-existing damage, previous repairs, aftermarket parts, or electrical components that fail from normal cleaning (e.g., already-damaged switches).

## 9. Satisfaction
If something in the package was missed, tell us within [24] hours and we'll return to correct it at no charge within [3] business days.

## 10. Maintenance plans (if applicable)
Plan price: $[___] per visit every [2 / 4] weeks, charged to the card on file after each visit. Either party may cancel with [7] days' notice. Missed visits caused by you (vehicle unavailable) may be charged [the late-cancellation fee].

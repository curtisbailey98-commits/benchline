# Mobile Detailing Client Intake Questions

---

## Contact
1. Name, mobile, email
2. Service address — home, office, or apartment? (Parking rules and water access differ)
3. How did you find us?

## Vehicle
4. Year, make, model, color
5. Size class: sedan/coupe · small SUV/crossover · mid-size SUV/crew-cab truck · full-size SUV/minivan · van
6. Interior: cloth, leather, or vinyl? Third row?
7. Any aftermarket parts, wraps, PPF (paint protection film), or ceramic coating already?

## Condition (ask for photos: driver seat, rear seat, carpets, 4 exterior corners)
8. When was it last detailed?
9. Pets? How much hair? (Hair woven into carpet takes much longer)
10. Kids? Car seats? Food spills?
11. Stains, odors (smoke, pet, mildew), or anything you're especially bothered by?
12. Exterior: swirl marks, scratches, water spots, tree sap, bugs, road tar?
13. Warning lights, broken trim, or electrical issues we should know about?

## Service
14. Which package interests you? (Share the menu link)
15. Add-ons: pet hair, extraction, headlight restoration, engine bay, odor treatment?
16. Is this for a special occasion or selling the car? (Timeline)
17. Interested in a maintenance plan every 2–4 weeks?

## Site
18. Can we park next to the vehicle with room to open all doors?
19. Covered parking or shade available?
20. Outdoor spigot and outlet access? (Or we bring our own)
21. HOA, apartment, or office rules about washing on site?

## Close

> "Thanks! Based on your photos, a [package] for your [size] is $[__] and takes about [#] hours. I'll do a quick walk-around with you before I start to note the car's current condition — standard for every car. Want me to book [day/time]?"

**Re-quote or decline when:** photos are refused and the car is described as "really bad," biohazards (vomit, blood, mold growth), heavy smoke odor on a budget price, or no legal place to wash.

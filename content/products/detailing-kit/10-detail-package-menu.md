# Detail Package Menu

A client-facing menu (good / better / best) you can paste into your website, booking page, or a one-page PDF. Prices below are the sheet defaults for a **sedan** — edit them in the **Package Menu** sheet and multiply by vehicle size.

## Packages

### Maintenance Wash — from $75
*For vehicles already on a regular schedule. About 1 hour.*
- Foam pre-wash and two-bucket hand wash
- Wheels, tires, and wheel wells cleaned; tires dressed
- Door jambs wiped
- Spray sealant topper
- Interior vacuum, dash and console wiped, glass inside and out

### Interior Detail — from $185
*About 2.5 hours.*
- Full vacuum including under seats, trunk, and crevices (compressed air)
- All plastics, vinyl, vents, and screens cleaned
- Cloth seats spot-treated **or** leather cleaned
- Door panels, door jambs, pedals
- Floor mats cleaned
- Glass inside
- Light fragrance (optional / fragrance-free on request)

### Exterior Detail — from $150
*About 2 hours.*
- Foam pre-wash, two-bucket hand wash
- Wheels deep-cleaned (faces, barrels as reachable), tires and wells
- Iron decontamination and clay bar treatment (paint feels smooth)
- Spray sealant (several months of protection)
- Exterior plastics and trim dressed; glass cleaned

### ⭐ Full Detail — from $295
*Interior Detail + Exterior Detail. About 4 hours. Our most popular package.*

### Paint Enhancement — from $450
*Full exterior detail plus a one-step machine polish. About 6 hours.*
- Removes light swirl marks and haze; noticeably improves gloss
- Sealant applied after polishing
- Deeper scratches (ones you can catch with a fingernail), rock chips, and clear-coat failure aren't removed

### Ceramic Coating — quoted per vehicle, from $900
*Only if you're trained and insured for it. Decon + polish + coating install; 1–2 days including cure time.*

### Menu-design tips (internal — delete before publishing)
1. **Three to four choices max.** Too many options slow decisions.
2. **Mark one package "most popular."** Most people choose the middle option.
3. **Show "from" prices** and the size table — no surprises at the car.
4. **Name outcomes, not chemicals** ("paint feels smooth" beats "clay bar decontamination").

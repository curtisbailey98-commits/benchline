# Benchline Mobile Detailing Trade Kit — Start Here

This kit applies the Benchline system to mobile auto detailing: a clear package menu priced by vehicle size, a condition inspection that protects you, and scripts that turn one-time details into maintenance clients.

## Set it up in one afternoon (about 3 hours)

| Step | Time | File | What to do |
|------|------|------|------------|
| 1 | 20 min | `02-detailing-pricing-calculator.xlsx` → **Your Numbers** | Enter pay goal, costs, billable hours → target hourly rate |
| 2 | 40 min | **Package Menu** sheet + `10-detail-package-menu.md` | Decide your 3–4 packages, base prices, and hours; set size and condition multipliers |
| 3 | 15 min | **Quote Builder** sheet | Quote 3 recent cars — do they hit your target? |
| 4 | 20 min | `11-vehicle-condition-inspection.md` | Print a pad or build it into your booking app; use it on every vehicle |
| 5 | 20 min | `05-...agreement.md` | Fill in terms; have a local attorney review |
| 6 | 20 min | `07-...intake.md` + `08-...text-scripts.md` | Booking form; save texts |
| 7 | 20 min | `06-...checklists.md` + `09-...calendar.md` | Print the loadout list; schedule seasonal offers |

## Before you take paid work

- **Insurance:** general liability plus garage keepers / care-custody-control coverage (covers the customer's vehicle while you're working on it). Ask your agent specifically.
- **Water runoff:** some cities and HOAs restrict wash water entering storm drains; consider a water reclamation mat or waterless/rinseless methods where required.
- This kit is not legal or insurance advice.

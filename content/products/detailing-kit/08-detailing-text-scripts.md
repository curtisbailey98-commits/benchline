# Mobile Detailing Text Scripts

---

## Booking

**Quote with menu**
> Hi [Name], thanks for the photos! For your [year model] ([size]), a [package] is $[__] and takes about [#] hours. Here's the full menu: [link]. I have [day/time] or [day/time] open — which works?

**Confirmation**
> You're booked: [package] on [day, date], arriving [window] at [address]. Please remove valuables and personal items, and make sure I can park next to the car. — [Your name], [Business]

**Day-before reminder**
> Hi [Name], see you tomorrow at [time]! Quick reminder: clear out personal items and leave me room to open all the doors. I bring my own water and power [or: I'll need the outdoor spigot].

**Weather**
> Hi [Name], rain's in the forecast for tomorrow. If you have a garage or covered spot I can still come; otherwise let's move to [option]. Which do you prefer?

**On my way**
> On my way — about [__] minutes out.

## After the detail

**Day-of**
> All done, [Name]! Before and after photos attached. The [coffee stain on the rear seat] came out about 90% — as we talked about, it had set in. Enjoy the car!

**Review request (after a positive reply)**
> Glad you love it! If you have a minute, a Google review with a photo of the car really helps: [link]. Thank you!

**Maintenance plan offer (2–3 days later)**
> Hi [Name], the easiest way to keep it looking like this is a maintenance wash every [2/4] weeks — $[__] per visit, same day each time, and it takes about an hour. Want me to set that up?

**Rebook one-time clients (6–8 weeks)**
> Hi [Name], it's been about two months since your detail. Want to get back on the schedule? I have openings on [days].

## Seasonal

**Spring (pollen)**
> Hi [Name], pollen season is rough on paint and cabin filters. I'm booking spring exterior details with a sealant for $[__]. Want a spot?

**Winter (road salt)**
> Hi [Name], road salt eats at undercarriages and carpets. My winter package includes an undercarriage rinse and salt-stain carpet extraction — $[__]. Interested?

**Holidays**
> Detailing gift cards are available for [holiday] — a full detail for someone who needs one. Want one?

## Problems

**Condition worse than photos (on site, before starting)**
> Hi [Name], I'm with the car now — the [pet hair / stains] are heavier than the photos showed. To do it right it's $[__] more (about [#] extra hours). I can do the full job with that adjustment, or stick to the original price and focus on [areas]. Your call.

**Client concern after service**
> Thanks for telling me. Can you send a photo? If it's something I missed, I'll come back and fix it at no charge.

*Benchline Mobile Detailing Trade Kit.*

# Mobile Detailing Pricing Guide

Detailing is sold as **packages**, delivered in **hours**, and adjusted by **vehicle size and condition**. Price all four explicitly and your quotes stop being guesses.

---

## 1. Your hourly target

In **Your Numbers** (defaults: $50k take-home, $14k costs, 46 weeks, 28 billable hours) the target is about **$72/hour**. Mobile detailing costs include your vehicle, water tank, generator or power, pressure washer, extractor, polisher, chemicals, and pads — plus drive time between jobs.

## 2. Build a menu of 3–4 packages

Clients choose faster from a short menu. Default menu (sedan prices, editable in the **Package Menu** sheet):

| Package | Base price (sedan) | Base hours |
|---------|-------------------|-----------|
| Maintenance wash | $75 | 1.0 |
| Interior detail | $185 | 2.5 |
| Exterior detail | $150 | 2.0 |
| **Full detail** | **$295** | **4.0** |
| Paint enhancement (1-step polish + sealant) | $450 | 6.0 |
| Ceramic coating (only if trained) | $900+ | 10.0 |

Full contents of each package are in `10-detail-package-menu.md`.

## 3. Vehicle size multipliers

| Size | Multiplier |
|------|-----------|
| Coupe / sedan | × 1.00 |
| Small SUV / crossover | × 1.15 |
| Mid-size SUV / crew-cab pickup | × 1.25 |
| Full-size SUV / minivan / 3-row | × 1.40 |
| Oversized / work van | × 1.60 |

## 4. Condition multipliers

| Condition | Multiplier | Examples |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Normal | × 1.0 | Regular use, light dirt |
| Dirty | × 1.2 | Heavy crumbs, stains on seats, months since last clean |
| Extreme | × 1.5 | Heavy pet hair everywhere, mold, smoke, vomit, sand/salt caked in carpets |

> Set the condition from photos **before** the appointment and confirm on the inspection form. If it's worse than the photos, re-quote before starting.

## 6. Worked example (sheet defaults)

Full detail · small SUV · normal condition · no add-ons
- $295 × 1.15 × 1.0 = $339 → **$340**
- 4.0 hr × 1.15 = **4.6 hours**
- Effective hourly ≈ **$74** (just above the $72 target)

Add pet hair removal (+$60, +0.75 hr): $400 for 5.35 hr ≈ $75/hr.

## 7. Travel

- Set a **free service radius** (10–15 miles is common) and a travel fee beyond it.
- Group appointments by area and day, just like a route business.
- Office parks and apartment complexes: book multiple cars at one location with a small multi-car discount — no driving between them.

## 8. Maintenance plans

The most stable revenue in detailing:
- **Every 2–4 weeks** maintenance wash + quick interior at a plan price ($60–90 per visit for a sedan)
- **Only after** an initial full or interior detail brings the car to a maintainable baseline
- Card on file, fixed day, skip rules in writing

## 9. Don't compete with the tunnel wash

Your price includes coming to them, hand work, and time. If a lead only compares you to a $15 automated wash, they're not your customer — offer the maintenance wash and move on.

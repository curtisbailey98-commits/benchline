# Booking & Reminder Scripts

---

**Booking confirmation**
> You're booked, [Name]! [Service] on [day, date], arriving between [window]. Total: $[__]. I'll text when I'm on the way. Reply here with any questions. — [Your name], [Business]

**Deposit request (if you take deposits)**
> To lock in [date], there's a $[__] deposit (applied to your total): [payment link]. Once that's in, you're all set!

**Prep instructions (2–3 days before)**
> Hi [Name], looking forward to [day]! To help things go smoothly: [1–3 prep items, e.g., clear counters / move cars / unlock the gate / remove valuables].

**Reminder (day before)**
> Hi [Name], reminder: I'll see you tomorrow between [window] for [service]. Reply C to confirm, or let me know if anything's changed.

**On my way**
> On my way! About [__] minutes out.

**Running late (send before the window starts)**
> Hi [Name], I'm running about [__] minutes behind — my last job took longer than planned. I can be there by [time]. Still work for you? Sorry about that.

**Weather delay**
> Hi [Name], the forecast for [day] is [rain / storms / freezing], which means I can't do a quality job. Can we move to [option 1] or [option 2]?

**Client needs to reschedule (reply)**
> No problem! I have [option 1] or [option 2]. Which works better?

**No-show / no access (on site)**
> Hi [Name], I'm here for your [time] appointment but [can't get in / no one's answering]. I'll wait until [time + 15]. After that, the [lockout/no-show] fee in our policy applies and we'll reschedule.

**Job complete (day-of)**
> All done, [Name]! [One specific thing you did]. If anything isn't right, text me a photo within 24 hours and I'll make it right. Thanks for having me!

**Payment (if not collected on site)**
> Here's your invoice for today: [link]. Thanks again!

**Recurring schedule confirmation**
> You're set up for [service] every [frequency] on [day], starting [date]. Your card on file will be charged $[__] after each visit. Text me anytime you need to skip or reschedule (24 hours' notice please).

# Review & Referral Scripts

**Rules:** ask only after a clear win, never while an issue is open, never offer discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews (it violates Google's policies and, in some cases, FTC rules), and never write reviews for clients.

---

## Step 1 — check satisfaction first
> Hi [Name], how's everything looking after [today / yesterday]? Anything you'd like done differently next time?

If there's any issue → fix it first (see 06-tough-conversations). If they're happy → Step 2.

## Step 2 — the review ask
> So glad to hear it! If you have a minute, a Google review really helps other [homeowners / neighbors / car owners] find someone reliable: [review link]. No pressure — thanks either way!

**Variation — mention a detail**
> Thanks, [Name]! If you're up for it, a quick Google review mentioning [the oven / the driveway / the pet hair] would mean a lot: [link].

**Variation — photo request (visual trades)**
> Thank you! If you leave a Google review, feel free to add a photo of the [driveway / car / yard] — it helps people see the difference: [link].

## Step 3 — one reminder (only if they said they would)
> Hi [Name], here's that review link again in case it got buried: [link]. Thanks so much!

## Replying to reviews (on Google)

**Positive**
> Thank you, [First name]! It was a pleasure [cleaning your home / washing your driveway]. See you [next time / in the spring].

**Negative (stay calm, short, take it offline)**
> Hi [First name], I'm sorry your experience didn't meet expectations. I'd like to make it right — please text me at [phone] so we can sort it out.

## Referral asks

**After a review or compliment**
> Thank you! If any friends or neighbors need [service], I'd really appreciate you passing my number along. Referrals from clients like you are how I grow.

**Neighborhood density**
> Hi [Name], I'll be on your street on [day] and have room for one or two more [yards / homes]. If a neighbor has mentioned needing [service], feel free to share my number.

**Referral thank-you**
> Hi [Name], [Referred client] booked with me and mentioned you sent them — thank you! I really appreciate it.

*(If you offer a referral credit, make it a fixed, disclosed amount for successful referrals — not tied to reviews.)*

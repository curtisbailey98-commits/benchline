# Quote Follow-Up Sequence

Most quotes aren't rejected — they're forgotten. A short, polite sequence recovers a meaningful share of them. Stop the sequence the moment they reply.

---

| When | Purpose | Script |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Same day** (with quote) | Deliver + next step | *"Hi [Name], here's your quote for [job]: [link]. Total $[__]. I have [day] open — reply YES and I'll book it."* |
| **Day 1** | Questions? | *"Hi [Name], just making sure the quote came through okay. Any questions about what's included?"* |
| **Day 3** | Offer an option | *"Hi [Name], checking in on the [job] quote. If budget's a factor, I can do [smaller scope] for $[__]. Want me to hold [day] for you?"* |
| **Day 7** | Close the loop | *"Hi [Name], I'll close out your quote at the end of the week so I can open the slot to others. If you'd still like it done, just reply and I'll get you scheduled. Thanks for considering me!"* |
| **Day 30** (optional, seasonal work) | Timing check | *"Hi [Name], still thinking about [job]? [Season-relevant reason, e.g., 'Spring schedule is filling up']. Happy to update your quote."* |

## Email versions (for clients who prefer email)

**Day 1 — Subject: Your [service] quote from [Business]**
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for the chance to quote your [job]. Your quote is attached / linked here: [link].
>
> **Total:** $[__]
> **Includes:** [3 bullet points]
> **Next available:** [day/date]
>
> Reply to this email or text me at [phone] to book. Happy to answer any questions.
>
> [Your name], [Business]

**Day 7 — Subject: Closing out your quote**
> Hi [Name],
>
> I haven't heard back about the [job] quote, so I'll close it out at the end of this week. If you'd still like the work done — now or later — just reply and I'll update it for you.
>
> Thanks again for considering [Business].
>
> [Your name]

## When they say no
> Thanks for letting me know, [Name] — I appreciate the reply. If anything changes or you need [service] down the road, I'm here.

(Then add them to the tracker with a 90-day "next touch" for seasonal services.)

## When they say "too expensive"
> Totally understand. Would it help if I [removed X / did it in two visits / did the most important part first] for $[__]?

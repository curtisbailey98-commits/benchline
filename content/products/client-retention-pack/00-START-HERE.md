# Benchline Client Retention Text Pack — Start Here

A library of short, human text scripts for every stage of the client relationship — plus a simple tracker so you know who to text this week. Works for any home-service trade.

## Set up in 45 minutes

1. **Save your top 10 scripts as phone shortcuts** (iPhone: Settings → General → Keyboard → Text Replacement; Android: Gboard → Dictionary → Personal dictionary) or as canned replies in your scheduling/invoicing app. Start with: lead reply, quote follow-up day 1 and 3, booking confirmation, on-my-way, running late, day-of thank-you, review ask, rebook, win-back 60-day.
2. **Replace every [bracket]** with your name, business, and links (review link, booking link).
3. **Open `07-client-tracker.csv`** in Google Sheets or Excel. Add every client from the last 12 months.
4. **Block 15 minutes every Monday** to sort the tracker by "next_touch_date" and send the week's texts.

## The rules that make texts work
- **Consent:** only text people who gave you their number for service. Honor "stop" immediately. For marketing blasts to many people, look up the TCPA and your state's texting rules — these scripts are designed for one-to-one conversations.
- **One ask per text.** Never stack a review request and an upsell.
- **Sound like you.** Edit the scripts until you'd actually say them.
- **Timing:** 8 AM–8 PM local time, never on Sunday mornings unless they text first.
- **Speed wins leads.** Reply to new inquiries within 15 minutes when you can — the first operator to respond often gets the job.

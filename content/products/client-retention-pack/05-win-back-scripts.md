# Win-Back Scripts

Clients rarely leave on purpose — they get busy, forget, or try someone cheaper. A single friendly text often brings them back. Use the tracker (`07-client-tracker.csv`) to find clients whose last service was 30, 60, or 90+ days ago.

---

## Recurring client who stopped booking

**30 days**
> Hi [Name], it's [Your name] from [Business]. I noticed it's been about a month since your last [service] — want me to get you back on the schedule? I have [day] open.

**60 days**
> Hi [Name], hope all's well! I've got a couple of openings on [day] in your area if you'd like a [service]. Happy to pick up where we left off.

**90+ days**
> Hi [Name], it's been a while! Just checking in — if there was anything about my service that wasn't right, I'd genuinely like to know. And if you'd like to book again, I'd love to have you back.

## One-time client (never rebooked)

**Seasonal hook**
> Hi [Name], [Your name] from [Business] — I [cleaned your home / washed your house / detailed your car] last [season]. [Season] is a great time for [related service]. Want me to put you on the schedule?

**Anniversary**
> Hi [Name], it's been about a year since your [service]. Want me to schedule this year's at the same price as last time?

## If they reply that they went elsewhere
> Totally understand — thanks for letting me know! If you ever need a backup, I'm here.

## If they reply with a complaint
> Thank you for telling me — I'm sorry about that. I'd like to make it right. [Specific fix / offer to come back and redo]. Would that help?

## If they don't reply
Mark "no response" in the tracker with a next touch in 90 days (seasonal) or 6 months. **Maximum 3 win-back texts per client per year.** Then stop.

## Holiday / seasonal broadcast (send individually, not group texts)
> Hi [Name], I'm booking [holiday / spring / fall] [service] now and wanted to offer past clients first pick of dates. Want me to hold one for you?

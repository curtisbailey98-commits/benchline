# Tough Conversations

Calm, short, specific. Move complicated conversations to a phone call, then confirm in writing.

---

## Complaints

**First response (within a few hours)**
> Thank you for telling me, [Name], and I'm sorry it wasn't right. Could you send me a photo? I want to fix it.

**Offering the fix**
> I can come back [day/time] to take care of [issue] at no charge. Does that work?

**When the complaint isn't valid (pre-existing / out of scope)**
> I understand the frustration. I checked my before photos — that [stain/scratch] was there before I started and it's noted on our walk-through. I did [what you did]. I'm happy to try [specialty option] for $[__] if you'd like.

**After the fix**
> All fixed! Thanks for your patience, [Name]. Let me know if anything else comes up.

## Late payment

**Friendly (day 7)**
> Hi [Name], a quick reminder that the invoice for [job] ($[__]) is still open: [link]. Thanks!

**Firmer (day 14)**
> Hi [Name], the [job] invoice from [date] ($[__]) is now past due. Can you let me know when to expect payment? Per our agreement, a late fee applies after [date].

**Pausing service (recurring)**
> Hi [Name], your account has a past-due balance of $[__]. I'm pausing your [service] until it's settled — here's the link: [link]. As soon as it's paid I'll get you right back on the schedule.

## Price increase
> Hi [Name], a heads-up: starting [date], your [service] will be $[new] (from $[old]). My costs for [fuel/insurance/supplies] have gone up and I want to keep the same quality. Thank you for being a client!

## Scope creep ("while you're here…")
> Happy to! That's not on today's list, so it'd be $[__] extra. Want me to add it?

## Client asks for a discount
> I appreciate you asking. My prices are already set to cover doing the job right, so I don't discount — but I could [reduce scope] for $[__] if that helps.

## Client is repeatedly late / cancels last minute
> Hi [Name], I've had to wait or reschedule a few times recently. To keep my schedule fair for everyone, I'll be applying the late-cancellation fee going forward, per our policy. I hope that makes sense — I value having you as a client.

## Ending the relationship (firing a client)

Sometimes a client costs more than they pay: constant re-do requests, disrespect, late payment, unsafe conditions. End it politely, finish any paid work, and don't argue.

> Hi [Name], after some thought I've decided I'm not the best fit for your [home / property] going forward. My last visit will be [date]. I'd suggest [a couple of options / looking for another local provider]. Thank you for the opportunity, and I wish you all the best.

**If they ask why (keep it short, no blame)**
> I've found I'm not able to meet your expectations the way I'd like to, and I think you'll be happier with someone else.

## Safety
If you ever feel unsafe on a job, leave and text afterward:
> Hi [Name], I had to leave today's appointment early. I'll be in touch about next steps.

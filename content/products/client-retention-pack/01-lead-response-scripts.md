# Lead Response Scripts

Goal of the first reply: **answer fast, ask the 1–2 questions you need to quote, and suggest a next step.**

---

**Web form / online inquiry**
> Hi [Name], this is [Your name] with [Business] — thanks for reaching out about [service]! To get you an accurate price, could you send [2–3 photos of X / the address / the size]? I can usually have a quote to you today.

**Missed call (auto-reply or within 15 minutes)**
> Hi, this is [Your name] with [Business]. Sorry I missed your call — I'm on a job. What can I help you with? Text is the fastest way to reach me.

**Referral**
> Hi [Name], [Referrer] passed along your number — thanks for reaching out! [Referrer]'s a great client of mine. What are you looking to have done?

**Repeat client**
> Hi [Name]! Great to hear from you. What do you need this time? I can probably fit you in [timeframe].

**After-hours inquiry (next morning, first thing)**
> Morning [Name] — got your message last night about [service]. I'd be glad to help. [Question needed to quote]?

**Lead outside your service area**
> Thanks so much for thinking of me, [Name]. Unfortunately [town] is outside the area I cover, so I can't get there reliably. I'd suggest [searching for local operators / a name you trust]. Best of luck!

**Lead that's not a fit (job type you don't do)**
> Thanks for reaching out! That's not a service I offer, so I don't want to waste your time — a [licensed electrician / roofer / etc.] would be the right call. If you ever need [what you do], I'd love to help.

**Price shopper ("How much do you charge?")**
> Great question — it depends on [size / condition / scope]. Most [2-bed cleans / small driveways / sedan full details] land between $[__] and $[__]. If you send [photos/details], I'll give you an exact price.

**Ask for booking once you have enough info**
> Thanks for the photos! For [scope], it's $[__]. I have [day/time] or [day/time] open. Want me to grab one for you?

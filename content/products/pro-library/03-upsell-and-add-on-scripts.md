# Upsell & Add-On Scripts

An upsell done right is just telling the customer about something they'd probably want if they knew you offered it. These scripts are short, specific, and easy to say no to.

**Rules**
- Offer, don't push. One offer per visit.
- Tie it to something you noticed ("I saw…"), not a sales quota.
- Always give the price up front.
- Write every accepted add-on on the estimate or invoice before you do it.

---

## When to offer

| Moment | Why it works |
|--------|--------------|
| On the estimate (as options) | They're already deciding — let them pick good / better / best |
| During the walk-around | You're pointing at the problem together |
| At job completion | They just saw your work and trust you most |
| In the follow-up text | Low pressure; they can reply later |

---

## General scripts

**Good / better / best on the estimate**
> Option A — [basic scope]: $[__]
> Option B — [basic + most-requested add-on]: $[__] (most customers pick this)
> Option C — [full scope]: $[__]

Only say "most customers pick this" once it's actually true for you.

**"I noticed…" during the job**
> While I was [doing X], I noticed [Y]. I can take care of it today for $[__], or leave it for next time — totally up to you.

**At completion**
> Everything's done. One thing for next time: [add-on] would [benefit]. It's $[__] if you want me to add it to your next visit.

**Recurring plan**
> A lot of my customers put this on a schedule so they don't have to think about it. Every [interval] is $[__] a visit — [small benefit: priority scheduling / locked rate]. Want me to set that up?

**If they say no**
> No problem at all. I'll make a note in case you change your mind.

---

## Trade-specific add-ons

**Cleaning**
> I can do inside the fridge and oven while I'm here — that's $[__] extra and adds about [time].
> Want me to add interior windows on your next clean? $[__].

**Lawn care**
> Your lawn's pretty compacted in the back — aeration this fall would really help. It's $[__] for your yard.
> I can add hedge trimming to next week's visit for $[__].

**Pressure washing**
> The driveway came out great. Want me to do the front walk and steps to match? $[__].
> I can apply a sealer on the deck once it's dry — $[__], and it'll hold up much longer.

**Mobile detailing**
> The paint's pretty contaminated — a clay bar and sealant would make it smoother and easier to wash. $[__] for this size.
> A lot of my regulars do a maintenance wash every two weeks for $[__]. Want me to put you on the schedule?

**Handyman**
> While I'm here with the ladder, want me to change the smoke detector batteries? $[__].
> I can knock out the other items on your list today — the [item] and [item] would be $[__] together.

---

## Track what works

Keep a simple tally: add-on offered, accepted (yes/no), price. After a month, drop the offers nobody takes and put the popular ones on your estimate as standard options.

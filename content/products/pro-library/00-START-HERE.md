# Benchline Pro Library

Thanks for joining Benchline Pro. This library is where Pro tools live. Download the latest version from your Benchline account any time your Pro access is active — the ZIP is rebuilt whenever we add or update something.

## What's in the library today

| File | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| `01-seasonal-pricing-planner.xlsx` | Plan your year: demand by month, when to raise prices, projected revenue, and how much to save in busy months to cover slow ones |
| `02-seasonal-pricing-playbook.md` | How to use the planner, when and how to raise prices, and what to do in slow months |
| `03-upsell-and-add-on-scripts.md` | Plain-language scripts for offering add-ons, upgrades, and recurring plans without being pushy |
| `CHANGELOG.md` | What was added or updated, and when |

## How Pro works

- **Billing:** $29/month, renews automatically each month until you cancel. Cancel anytime from your Benchline account (Manage billing) or through the Benchline contact page. You keep access through the end of the period you paid for.
- **Bundle buyers:** if you bought Core + 3 Months Pro, your Pro access runs for three months from purchase and does not auto-renew.
- **New tools:** we add tools over time — examples of what we plan to add include pricing calculators, seasonal tools, customer and upsell scripts, quote templates, expense and profit tools, service-specific workflows, and operational checklists. We don't promise a specific file on a specific date.
